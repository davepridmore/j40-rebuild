## Decision

Do not buy imported pre-bent FJ40 brake-line kits unless the user explicitly approves it. They are the expensive path, and they may still be wrong for this truck if the front disc conversion, RHD/LHD layout, booster/proportioning setup, or chassis routing differs.

Use local brake-pipe fabrication first:

1. Remove/photograph old lines only when the truck is safely staged and ports can be capped.
2. Keep old hard lines as bend templates.
3. Buy or quote `4.75 mm / 3/16 in` brake-rated Bundy steel or CuNi/Cunifer tube.
4. Confirm Toyota-style double/inverted flare, tube-nut thread, and seat from a removed old line and opened port.
5. Have the local shop copy the old lines and pressure/leak-test after installation.
