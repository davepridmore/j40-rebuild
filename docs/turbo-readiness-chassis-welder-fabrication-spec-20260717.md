# J40 2H Turbo-Readiness Chassis Welder Fabrication Spec — Rev A

Date: 2026-07-17
Package: `turbo_readiness_chassis_rev_a`
Owner: chassis/body welding workstream
Status: released for inspection, measurement, cooling-stack structure and conditional vehicle-side provisions only

> **Superseded as a current welder handout on 2026-07-19.** Do not send this detailed planning document to the chassis welder now. Use [chassis-welder-steering-turbo-component-first-instruction-20260719.md](chassis-welder-steering-turbo-component-first-instruction-20260719.md). The practical decision is to perform no conversion-specific welding until the complete J60 steering hardware and selected turbo/manifold components are physically present for trial fitting. Retain this document only as later engineering/inspection reference.

## 1. Purpose

Prepare the J40 chassis/front structure, with the Toyota 2H **remaining installed**, for a later conservative turbo installation without guessing the turbo, downpipe, intercooler, airbox or pipe positions. Engine removal is not part of this work.

This release permits the welder to complete the two-sided front cooling-stack structure, preserve the future hot-side corridor, repair or relocate endangered line supports, and install approved removable-interface points after a physical mock-up. It does **not** release a turbo manifold, downpipe, exhaust, sump modification, charge pipes or turbo support.

All dimensions are in millimetres. Dimensions marked `TRANSFER` must be measured from the actual assembled vehicle and entered on the job card before cutting or welding.

## 2. Vehicle State and Required Mock-Up

The engine is attached and remains in the vehicle throughout this chassis-welder package. Use the installed engine, its mounts and its surrounding equipment as the measurement datum. Do not loosen or remove the engine simply to improve welding access. If a released joint cannot be prepared and welded safely with the engine installed, record it as `HELD — ACCESS BLOCKED`; do not improvise a different bracket position.

No conditional turbo-related tab, boss or hanger may be positioned until all of the following are physically present or represented by rigid templates:

- installed 2H engine on its current mounts, with mount condition and fastener security checked; if the mounts will be replaced, final turbo-dependent positions remain held until the replacement mounts are fitted;
- gearbox/transfer on final mounts;
- steering column/box/shaft and clutch/brake hardware;
- firewall, floor edge, bonnet, inner wings and front panel;
- radiator, fan and shroud;
- A/C compressor and condenser envelope;
- selected intercooler or a rigid full-size intercooler template;
- selected turbo/manifold assembly or a rigid flange-and-housing template;
- proposed downpipe centreline represented by rigid tube/wire template;
- suspension at static ride height, with engine movement direction identified.

Photograph the complete mock-up from front, rear, both sides and above with a ruler visible before marking any final vehicle-side provision.

### Work that can proceed with the engine installed

- Survey and label the hot-side corridor.
- Measure current engine-to-chassis, steering, firewall and front-stack clearances.
- Complete accessible cooling-stack structure under its existing release.
- Repair approved vehicle-side line/loom tabs outside blocked or heat-sensitive areas.
- Template future hanger, shield and support locations without welding them before their hold points close.

The later turbo oil-return operation requires removal of the **sump from the installed engine**, not removal of the engine from the chassis. That operation remains outside this chassis-welder release.

## 3. Materials and Hardware

| Use | Specification | Minimum thickness / size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cooling-stack full-height uprights | New mild-steel angle; `50 x 50 x 4` preferred | `4` wall; `40 x 40 x 4` minimum only if the vehicle envelope requires it |
| Cooling-stack chassis/front-support saddles | New mild-steel plate or formed angle | `4` |
| Exhaust-hanger chassis tabs | New mild-steel plate, radiused corners | `4` |
| Heat-shield, line-clip, airbox and pipe-support interface tabs | New mild-steel plate, radiused corners | `3` |
| Removable brackets fabricated later | Mild steel or 5052-H32 aluminium as separately released | Not part of this chassis-welder release |
| Threaded removable interfaces | Weld nuts or captive nuts | M8 x 1.25, property class 8 minimum; use M6 x 1.0 only for light line clips/heat shields |
| Structural through-bolts | Zinc-plated class 8.8 minimum | M8 or M10 as transferred from the approved joint design |
| Box-section crush tubes | Seamless/structural steel tube | ID gives 0.5–1.0 clearance over bolt; length equals measured box width within +0/−0.5 |
| Exhaust isolators | Heat-rated automotive rubber | Selected after exhaust mock-up; not welded or loaded during this release |

Do not use rusty stock, reinforcing bar, thin body sheet, slotted domestic angle, ungraded fasteners or rubber cut from tyres.

## 4. Released Work

### TRC-001 — Survey and preserve the hot-side corridor

1. Record the right and left chassis rails, engine-mount brackets, steering, brake/clutch pipes, fuel route, wiring route, A/C route, firewall and floor edge.
2. Mark every existing tab or bracket in the proposed turbo/downpipe zone as `KEEP`, `REPAIR`, `RELOCATE`, `REMOVE NON-BASELINE` or `UNKNOWN`.
3. Do not remove an `UNKNOWN` item.
4. Preserve a continuous mock-up corridor from the turbine outlet to the point where the exhaust can pass rearward without contacting a fixed vehicle part through the engine-movement envelope.

Acceptance: labelled photographs and a hand sketch with measured nearest clearances; no unidentified bracket removed.

### TRC-002 — Complete the two-sided cooling-stack structure

Follow the existing front-cooling-stack Rev A package. Use the existing approved upright as the transfer datum and add the mirrored full-height upright in `50 x 50 x 4` mild-steel angle where packaging permits.

- Prefer bolt-through saddles with crush tubes over welding directly to a boxed chassis rail.
- Uprights must be straight within `2` over their full measured length and mutually parallel within `3`.
- Radiator, condenser and intercooler must remain separately removable.
- No core, tank, fin, radiator tab or condenser tab may carry another component.
- Intercooler interfaces are bolt-on and rubber-isolated. Their hole centres remain `TRANSFER` from the selected core/template.
- Maintain the existing condenser-to-radiator planning air gap of `10–15` unless the full stack test releases another dimension.

Acceptance: cooling-stack inspection checklist passed; radiator/fan/shroud and front-panel service removal demonstrated without cutting welded metal.

### TRC-003 — Line and loom protection provisions

Relocate a brake, clutch, fuel, A/C or electrical route only when the mock-up proves it enters the hot-side/downpipe corridor.

- Use `3` mm steel tabs with minimum `8` mm edge distance from hole edge to tab edge.
- Light clip interfaces may use M6 x 1.0; multi-line or shield interfaces use M8 x 1.25.
- Round external corners to at least R3 and deburr all edges.
- Retain the original line material and bend radius unless a separate pipe/hose release changes it.
- Metal lines must not be pulled into alignment by their clips.
- Final clips require rubber lining or the correct automotive isolator; no bare steel clamp directly on a brake, fuel or A/C line.

Acceptance: each route is clipped without preload, has no sharp-edge contact and remains accessible for replacement.

### TRC-004 — Conditional exhaust-hanger interface tabs

Install only after the turbine outlet, rigid downpipe template, flex-joint position and complete engine/body mock-up are approved.

- Tab stock: `4` mm mild steel.
- Use a removable M8/M10 bracket interface or a transferred hanger-pin design; do not weld the exhaust pipe directly to the chassis.
- Place the first chassis-side hanger downstream of the flex joint.
- Exhaust weight must be supported independently of the turbo and manifold.
- Hanger orientation must allow engine movement without pulling the downpipe sideways.
- Final hole/pin centre is `TRANSFER`; mark on the mock-up and photograph before drilling.

Acceptance: with the exhaust represented at full weight, no load is transferred to the manifold/turbo, and the system can be removed without cutting.

### TRC-005 — Conditional heat-shield interfaces

After the hot-side/downpipe envelope is approved, install only the vehicle-side attachment points needed for removable shields.

- Use `3` mm steel tabs with M6 x 1.0 or M8 x 1.25 captive threads.
- Provide at least three non-collinear attachment points per independent shield where the geometry permits.
- Shield panels and stand-offs are a later turbo-fabricator item.
- Do not bridge the moving engine/turbo assembly to the fixed chassis/body with one rigid shield.
- Keep attachment points accessible with ordinary hand tools.

Acceptance: cardboard shield template installs/removes with bonnet, wing and service components represented.

### TRC-006 — Conditional supports for airbox, breather and charge plumbing

Provide vehicle-side threaded interfaces only after the selected components and rigid route templates are present.

- Use `3` mm steel tabs with M8 x 1.25 captive threads.
- Final brackets must be bolt-on and rubber-isolated where they carry an airbox or breather separator.
- Charge-pipe supports must include a compliant joint and must not restrain engine movement.
- Nothing may place static weight on the compressor housing or intercooler end tanks.
- Final positions and hole centres are `TRANSFER`.

Acceptance: templates can be removed for servicing; bonnet, steering, fan, belts, radiator cap/drain and battery service paths remain clear.

## 5. Welding Controls

- Disconnect and remove batteries, electronic modules, fuel containers and vulnerable trim before welding.
- The engine remains installed. Do not weld if diesel fuel, engine oil, brake fluid, cleaning solvent or oily residue is present in or can drain into the hot-work area; clean the leak and re-inspect first.
- Disconnect the alternator and any fitted electronic controller/sender that could be damaged by welding current. Isolate loose cable ends so they cannot touch earth.
- Fit clean fire-resistant blankets and rigid metal guards between the weld zone and the engine, injection equipment, hoses, wiring, belts, seals, glass and painted panels. Do not place a blanket directly on an oily surface or hot exhaust part.
- Seal the intake, breather and any open engine port against grinding dust and weld spatter using clean removable plugs/covers. Record and count every temporary plug before and after work.
- Attach the welding return clamp to clean bare parent metal on the same component being welded and as close to the weld as practical. The current path must not pass through the engine, gearbox, bearings, steering, brake lines or wiring grounds.
- Keep an appropriate extinguisher immediately available and assign a second person as fire watch during hot work and for at least `30 minutes` after the final weld/grind operation.
- After hot work, inspect the engine bay from above and below for smouldering material, damaged hoses/loom, spatter and forgotten covers before reconnecting electrical equipment.
- Strip paint, rust, seam sealer and oil at least `25` around every weld zone.
- Weld only to sound metal of verified thickness. Stop and report thinning, perforation or cracking.
- Do not weld across chassis-flange edges, suspension brackets, stamped identification or an existing crack termination without a separately approved structural repair.
- Prefer short balanced welds on opposing sides of a bracket to limit distortion. Do not continuously seam-weld a small tab to thin parent metal unless its approved joint drawing requires it.
- Do not weld closed box sections without confirmed drain/vent and corrosion-treatment access.
- Grind only sharp projections and interference; do not grind structural welds flush for appearance.
- After cooling, remove spatter, inspect both sides where accessible, clean, prime bare steel and restore seam/cavity protection only after inspection sign-off.

## 6. Mandatory Hold Points

| Hold | Stop condition | Release authority/evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Before deleting or relocating any existing bracket/route | Owner/workshop labels its function and approves photo |
| H2 | Before cutting/drilling cooling-stack interfaces | Actual component or rigid template fitted; dimensions entered |
| H3 | Before welding to chassis/front structure | Bare sound parent metal photographed; joint type approved |
| H4 | Before exhaust-hanger tab positioning | Turbo/downpipe/flex and body/engine mock-up approved |
| H5 | Before heat-shield or airbox/pipe interface positioning | Full-size templates installed and service clearances accepted |
| H6 | Before primer, Raptor or cavity wax | All welds, holes, crush tubes, threads and removability checks passed |

No verbal “approximately here” instruction releases a hold. Use a marked photograph or signed job-card row.

For every welded item, H3 evidence must also show the engine-installed protection, nearby welding-return clamp position and unobstructed extinguisher access.

## 7. Explicitly Excluded Work

The chassis welder must not under this release:

- fabricate, flip, cut or weld the exhaust manifold;
- fabricate the downpipe or complete exhaust;
- weld a turbo support to the chassis;
- weld or modify the sump;
- remove or loosen the installed engine for access;
- drill the engine oil gallery or fabricate oil lines;
- fabricate fixed charge pipes;
- choose or permanently mount an intercooler without the selected core/template;
- weld the exhaust rigidly to the chassis;
- add generic unused tabs;
- final-coat a held weld zone before inspection.

## 8. Final Acceptance

The chassis-welder portion is complete only when:

1. every scope row is signed `PASS`, `NOT REQUIRED` or `HELD — NOT FABRICATED`;
2. all transferred dimensions are recorded in millimetres;
3. all welded interfaces are photographed before and after coating;
4. threaded interfaces accept the specified bolt by hand for full engagement;
5. no hose, line or loom is preloaded or contacts a sharp edge;
6. radiator, condenser, intercooler template, fan/shroud and front panel remain independently removable;
7. future turbo/downpipe work can proceed without cutting newly completed structural work;
8. excluded turbo-engine fabrication remains untouched.
9. all temporary intake/port covers, fire blankets and guards are removed and counted, and disconnected electrical equipment is restored only after the final inspection.
