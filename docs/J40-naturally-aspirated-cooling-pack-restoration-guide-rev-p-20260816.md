# J40 Naturally Aspirated Cooling + A/C Restoration Guide — Rev P

**Issue:** 16 August 2026

**Status:** controlled shop guide; final dimensions, structural fabrication release and installed performance release remain **HOLD** until the actual parts are measured

**Applies to:** R0 copper/brass radiator, C0 full-face A/C condenser, G0 expanded-mesh grille/stone guard, one FS front A/C pusher, FL rear radiator puller/shroud, the two loose radiator-arm blanks, new chassis-connector ends, new guard holders, new R0 holder system, hoses, fittings, wiring and finishes

**Decision:** naturally aspirated Toyota 2H; no turbocharger, no intercooler/K0, no charge-air pipework and no second small front fan

Rev P supersedes Rev O. It retains Rev O’s shortened-arm and holder design, and replaces the former chained centring rule with one common vehicle-longitudinal centre datum for the complete G0 guard and single FS front-fan installation. Rev O recorded the owner’s 15 August correction that the radiator uprights/arms are **loose and not attached to the chassis**. The earlier July description of a completed welded radiator arm remains historical progress evidence, not the current as-built state. Rev P therefore retains the deletion of X2 adapter brackets and the pair of shortened arms made directly to the real chassis connectors.

Photographs and generated images identify parts and intent; they are not dimensioned drawings. The old 410 mm / 4 mm Rev A upright package is superseded and must not be used for final cutting.

## 1. Released architecture and load paths

The controlled air path is:

**fixed vehicle grille/body opening → centrally mounted G0 removable guard → one centrally mounted FS A/C pusher → full-face C0 condenser → R0 radiator → sealed FL rear puller/shroud → engine**

The radiator weight path is:

**R0 lower locators → two new R1 rubber saddles → X1-L/R saddle seats → X0 crossmember → shortened A0-L/R arm assemblies → verified chassis connector interfaces**

- R0 weight is carried only at its two lower locators. Tanks, seams, fins, soldered joints and upper ears carry no vertical weight.
- R3-U-L/R upper keepers locate and restrain R0 through sleeved EPDM joints; they must be neutral when tightened.
- G0, C0, FS and FL have independent removable carriers. No core hangs from another heat exchanger or from G0.
- G1/G2 hold only the sound perimeter frame of G0. They never clamp the mesh.
- One FS remains the controlled A/C fan. FL remains the radiator fan.
- **Central alignment is controlled directly by CL0 and must not be tolerance-stacked:** project the vehicle longitudinal centre plane (**VCL**) at the G0, FS and C0 mounting planes. The complete repaired G0 perimeter-frame outer-envelope centre, the complete mounted FS frame/rotor datum and the C0 usable-fin-field lateral centreline must each be within **±2 mm laterally of VCL**. Separately, G0 must be within **±2 mm X/Z** of the measured usable fixed vehicle-grille aperture centre, and the FS frame/rotor datum must be within **±2 mm X/Z** of the C0 usable-fin-field centre. The fixed vehicle grille/body opening is not G0: G0 is the separate removable expanded-mesh guard. Represent the complete guard and fan frames, tabs, guard, plug, cable bend and service envelopes; these clearance envelopes do not replace the perimeter-frame and frame/rotor centring datums. If the complete assemblies cannot meet both the direct VCL limits and the local aperture/C0 limits, stop and revise G1/G2, C1 or F1; do not release an offset installation by eye.

## 2. Shortened arm and connector specification

### 2.1 Controlled part definition

Fabricate two mirror-handed **A0-L/A0-R shortened connector-arm assemblies**. Each assembly comprises a verified straight arm section and its integral or drawing-released **A1 connector shoe/end fitting**.

- The lower end must match the actual chassis connector face, hole group, bearing plane and access envelope recorded by A0-D-L/R.
- The upper end must stop at the highest released functional interface needed by X0, R3-U or another explicitly assigned carrier. There must be no unused tall projection.
- Finished height is derived from fixed datums: `highest required interface Z − chassis connector bearing-plane Z`, with bend, edge-distance and tool-clearance allowances shown on the released drawing. **Do not copy 410 mm or scale a photograph.**
- A1 may be an integral formed shoe or a welded/bolted end fitting. The released drawing must state which; no loose spacer stack or improvised side tab is permitted.
- Use only the existing structural connector intended for this support. Do not attach to steering, suspension, bumper, tow points or thin/non-structural sheet.

### 2.2 Reworking the loose arm blanks

The loose existing pieces may be shortened on the bench only after both are identified and checked for material, actual thickness/section, straightness, corrosion, cracks, old holes and previous heat damage.

1. Make A0-D-L/R rigid connector templates and a full-size arm/card template.
2. Mark the connector bearing plane, X0 centreline, highest required upper interface and final cut line on each blank.
3. Cut only loose parts on the bench. Do not trim an installed chassis member.
4. Remove all redundant top returns, holes and unsupported projection from the finished design; do not leave a decorative post.
5. If a tubular section is cut, use the released closure/drain arrangement; do not create a sealed water trap.
6. Jig the pair from fixed left/right datums, add the drawing-released A1 connector ends/gussets, and weld only to the approved WPS.
7. Inspect weld profile and heat-affected zones, then trial-fit using the final fastener stack without forced alignment.

If the loose blanks fail inspection or cannot accept the connector geometry with adequate edge distance, make a new matched pair from certified weldable structural steel. Exact grade, section, wall/plate thickness, length, holes, bends, gussets, weld sizes, fasteners and torque remain structural-drawing **HOLD**.

## 3. New grille/stone-guard holder specification

G0 is the actual removable expanded-mesh guard shown in `20260802_173652_gp_XFyn0ruQ.jpg`. It is separate from the fixed vehicle grille/body opening. Its repaired perimeter frame—not the mesh—controls the holder and the CL0 guard datum.

### G1-L/R lower guard cradles

- Two mirror-handed removable cradles support the lower straight portions of the G0 perimeter frame through replaceable EPDM strips.
- Each cradle needs a formed return/lip that prevents fore/aft escape without pinching the mesh, plus drainage at the low point.
- The loaded contact must be broad enough not to dent the repaired frame. No point screw, self-tapper or through-core tie is permitted.

### G2-L/R upper guard keepers

- Two removable upper/side keepers prevent rattle and lift while allowing the complete guard to be withdrawn along the G0-H released removal vector.
- Use positive captive or locking hardware accessible with the grille installed or through the documented service sequence.
- Use replaceable EPDM anti-chafe at every frame contact and edge trim wherever a cut edge can meet the guard.

G1/G2 must mount to released front-support/A0 carrier points independently of R0, C0 and both fans. Exact quantity may change only if the G0-H map and signed drawing prove an equivalent stable, serviceable arrangement. Material, thickness, blank, bends, holes and fasteners remain drawing **HOLD**; folded coated mild steel is the default compatible shop route, with aluminium allowed only under a galvanically isolated released design.

Set G1/G2 as a mirror-handed pair wherever the measured interfaces permit. Their drawing must reference CL0 and hold the complete repaired G0 perimeter-frame outer-envelope centre—not merely its mesh insert—within ±2 mm laterally of VCL and within ±2 mm X/Z of the measured usable fixed vehicle-grille aperture centre.

## 4. R0 radiator-holder specification

R0 is shown black in the illustrative generated image; that finish and the depicted holders do not prove the actual as-built condition. Record the actual radiator first using R0-H and classify every ear/tab as original, later-added, repairable, replace, or unused.

### R0-E radiator-side ears/tabs

- Retain a sound existing side-rail ear only after cleaning and crack/thinning inspection.
- If an ear must be reproduced, the radiator shop must jig it from R0-H and attach it to a sound side rail/header support in the same approved load class—not to a tank skin, tube, fin pack or core face.
- All radiator-shop hot work is completed before the final pressure/flow test and thin radiator coating.
- No chassis fabricator may arc-weld a bracket to the copper/brass radiator.

### R3-U-L/R upper radiator keepers

- Two short removable vehicle-side keepers mate only with the R0-H released sound upper ears.
- Each joint uses a new automotive EPDM bush, fitted steel crush sleeve, broad washer, released zinc class 8.8 bolt and locknut.
- Sleeve length—not bolt torque against rubber—sets compression. Tightening must not lift, pull, rack or twist R0 or unload either R1 saddle.

### R3-L-L/R optional lower-side stabilisers

- Add these only if the R0-H map and full dry fit prove that lower side restraint is required after the two locators and upper keepers are installed.
- They may restrain fore/aft or lateral motion through sleeved EPDM, but must not carry radiator weight.
- Do not reproduce the photographed historical added leg. It is an old pattern/problem, not an approved support.

### F2 rear-fan/shroud holder

- F2 is an independent removable carrier aligned to R0’s side-rail/seal map. It carries FL and its shroud without loading the tanks, core or soldered ears.
- Seal the shroud perimeter to the radiator frame with closed-cell automotive EPDM while leaving fins, cap, drain, hoses and fasteners serviceable.

## 5. Mandatory measurement controls

Do not make final metal, buy final hoses, drill, weld or coat until these records are complete:

1. **A0-D-L/R:** rigid templates of both chassis connectors: fixed-datum XYZ, bearing faces, hole centres/diameters, section/condition, connector thickness, bolt approach, obstacles, drainage and removal path.
2. **B0:** rigid template of both released upper locating-hole axes and planes.
3. **S0:** rigid template of both R0 lower locators, saddle centres, locator diameters and installed R1 rubber height.
4. **CL0:** a common centre-datum record: VCL projected at the G0, FS and C0 mounting planes; usable fixed vehicle-grille aperture centre X/Z; repaired G0 perimeter-frame outer-envelope centre X/Z; C0 usable-fin-field centre X/Z; and complete mounted FS frame/rotor datum X/Z. Measure all from fixed vehicle datums with complete frames, tabs, guards, plugs, cable bends and service envelopes represented. Record direct G0-to-VCL, FS-to-VCL and C0-to-VCL lateral offsets independently before recording G0-to-aperture and FS-to-C0 X/Z offsets; no tolerance stacking is permitted.
5. **G0-H:** full removable guard perimeter/frame map: outside envelope, sound straight contact lengths, frame planes/holes, mesh clearance, fixed vehicle-grille clearance and removal vector.
6. **R0-H:** every radiator ear/tab/rail/locator interface: XYZ, plane, hole/slot, material/condition, original-versus-added classification, tank/core clearance and intended function.
7. **L0/MR:** X0 clear span including A0 end connections and filled/capped installed radiator mass.
8. **R0/C0/FS/FL maps:** complete envelopes, fittings, ports, tabs, motors, plugs, cable bends, seals and tool sweeps.
9. **Vehicle envelope:** grille, bonnet/latch, bumper, steering, suspension, tow structure, support steel, engine movement, hoses, A/C pipes, wiring and service tools.
10. **Opaque 1:1 dry fit:** actual parts, realistic rubbers, fasteners, hoses, wiring and service tools.

## 6. Step-by-step shop sequence

### Step 1 — Identify and baseline-test

Tag and photograph R0, C0, G0, FS, FL, both loose arm blanks and every bracket. Cap open ports. Record R0 pressure/flow, C0 specialist leak/contamination status, and FS/FL voltage, start/run current, rotation and airflow direction.

**Gate:** signed retain/repair/replace decisions.

### Step 2 — Measure connectors, holders and service envelopes

Produce A0-D-L/R, B0, S0, CL0, G0-H and R0-H. Record L0/MR and component/vehicle envelopes on one dated drawing from fixed chassis datums. Make full-size card/rigid templates and an opaque stack.

**Gate:** every proposed bracket has an assigned function and measured interface.

### Step 3 — Recore and restore R0

Baseline pressure/flow test; jig tanks, headers, necks, rails, ears and locators. Remove the tired core, clean/tin sound copper/brass with inhibited solder-compatible chemistry, install the selected naturally aspirated-duty core squarely and resolder without moving R0-H geometry. Repair/reproduce any released R0-E ear in the jig. Repeat pressure/flow tests, mask interfaces and apply only thin radiator-shop heat-exchanger coating.

**Gate:** square, pressure-tight, free-flowing, straight open fins, clean solder and written results.

### Step 4 — Restore C0

Use an automotive A/C shop. Clean with neutral aluminium-safe coil cleaner, low-pressure clean-side rinse and fin comb. Leak-test with dry nitrogen or forming gas at the applicable service pressure; never oxygen or wet shop air. Fit a new compatible receiver-drier and new HNBR seals/caps at final assembly.

**Gate:** capped clean ports, straight fins and written leak result.

### Step 5 — Restore G0 and qualify FS/FL

Jig/repair G0’s perimeter, preserve mesh open area and finish with thin satin/semi-gloss black. Retain exactly one complete FS pusher and FL puller/shroud only after frame, blade, bearing, plug, current, direction and airflow checks. Establish VCL directly at the G0, FS and C0 mounting planes, then record the repaired G0 perimeter-frame, C0 usable-fin-field and complete FS frame/rotor datums without chained measurements. Do not paint blades, motors, vents, labels, plugs or rubber.

**Gate:** safe current/direction, no crack/wobble/rub and credible installed airflow.

### Step 6 — Fabricate X0/X1 and shortened A0/A1 arms

Set the final R1 samples and S0 saddle centres, design X0 and X1, then cut/fabricate A0-L/R to A0-D-L/R and the highest required holder datum. A competent automotive structural reviewer must release vertical, fore/aft and lateral road loads, shock, vibration, fatigue, material/section, connector bearing, edge distance, welds, fasteners and torque. No new chassis holes, slotting, reaming, forced pull or washer-stack spacers.

**Gate:** signed structural drawing covers X0/X1/A0/A1 and both actual connector interfaces.

### Step 7 — Fabricate G1/G2, R3 and independent carriers

Set R0 naturally on R1, transfer B0/R0-H to R3-U, and make G1/G2 from G0-H and CL0 so the complete guard is centred directly on VCL as well as in the measured usable fixed vehicle-grille aperture. Make R3-L only if released. Fabricate C1, F1, F2 and E1 as independent removable carriers; F1 must centre the complete FS frame/rotor datum directly on VCL as well as on the C0 usable fin field. Use sleeved EPDM isolation and positive accessible fasteners.

**Gate:** all fabricated items are tagged and each heat exchanger/guard/fan remains independently removable.

### Step 8 — Bare-metal 1:1 dry fit

Assemble the full sequence with real rubbers, fasteners, plugs, bends, hoses, pipes and tools. Confirm both saddles share weight and R3-U stays neutral. Measure each direct lateral offset from VCL: G0 perimeter-frame outer-envelope centre ≤2 mm, complete FS frame/rotor datum ≤2 mm and C0 usable-fin-field centreline ≤2 mm. Then confirm G0 is within 2 mm X/Z of the usable fixed vehicle-grille aperture centre and FS is within 2 mm X/Z of the C0 usable-fin-field centre. Do not derive one result from another. Confirm the complete frames/tabs/guards/plug/cable/service envelopes clear; G0 removal clears the fixed grille and fan; FL seals/pulls rearward; bonnet/latch and engine movement clear.

**Gate:** owner and fabricator sign the bare-metal drawing and photo record.

### Step 9 — Structural proof and finish

After structural release, apply a **total static proof load of 2 × MR**, distributed between the two S0 saddle centres in the released loaded-radiator distribution, for **10 minutes**. The released fixture must avoid local point-loading. Fail for permanent set, crack, connector/fastener movement, saddle displacement or arm distortion. Then deburr, prepare and coat.

**Gate:** proof passes before paint.

### Step 10 — Plumbing, wiring and commissioning

Install measured new moulded coolant hoses, overflow hose, constant-tension clamps, approved cap, Toyota 2H thermostat/seal and one compatible coolant family. Complete A/C with new drier/HNBR seals. Give FS and FL separate sealed relay/fuse/earth branches. Heat-cycle, hot-idle A/C-test and road/load-log the naturally aspirated vehicle; reinspect after cool-down and the first loaded journey.

**Gate:** no leak, rub, boil, purge, progressive coolant rise, progressive A/C high-side escalation or witness-mark movement.

## 10. Release record

1. Component tests pass: R0 pressure/flow, C0 leak/cleanliness, FS/FL electrical/direction.
2. A0-D, B0, S0, CL0, G0-H, R0-H, L0/MR and all component/vehicle envelopes are signed; direct G0-to-VCL, FS-to-VCL and C0-to-VCL lateral offsets plus local G0-to-aperture and FS-to-C0 X/Z offsets each meet the 2 mm limits without tolerance stacking.
3. Structural drawing releases X0/X1/A0/A1, connectors, welds, fasteners, torque and proof fixture.
4. G1/G2 use only the G0 perimeter frame; R3 uses only released sound R0 ears/rails; no core load.
5. Opaque bare-metal dry fit and total 2 × MR proof pass.
6. Finish, hardware, plumbing and separate fan circuits pass inspection.
7. Hot-idle A/C, NA road/load cooling and first-journey reinspection pass.

Until all seven are signed, status remains **FINAL FIT + FABRICATION + PERFORMANCE RELEASE HOLD**.
