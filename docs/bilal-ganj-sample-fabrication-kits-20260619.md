# Bilal Ganj / Montgomery Road Sample Fabrication Kits

Date: 2026-06-19

Source table: `data/manual/bilal_ganj_sample_fabrication_kits_20260619.csv`

## Rule

Build four separate physical kits. Bag and label each kit. Do not mix brake hydraulic parts, handbrake cable parts, brake springs, and fuel hoses.

Old parts are patterns only. They stay with the project until the replacements are fitted, adjusted, and tested.

## Kits

| Kit | Take | Shop target | Acceptance rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BG-KIT-HB-001` rear handbrake splitter/equalizer cable | All rear cable pieces, equalizer/yoke hardware, clips, adjusters, springs, and route photos | Montgomery Road auto-control-cable maker first; Bilal Ganj only for missing metal yoke/equalizer hardware | New cable must pull both rear wheels evenly through a free-moving equalizer and release fully. |
| `BG-KIT-BRSPR-001` rear brake/handbrake springs | All rear drum springs, hold-downs, adjuster springs, lever clips, side labels, and comparison kit contents | Bilal Ganj brake hardware stalls / Montgomery Road brake parts shops | Replace every weak/rusty spring; match free length, wire diameter, coil OD, and hook direction. |
| `BG-KIT-BRHOSE-001` brake flex hoses | Front left, front right, and rear center hose assemblies with fittings, clips, brackets/photos, and route photos | Brake hose crimp shop, not a generic rubber shop | Must be complete crimped hydraulic brake assemblies rated `DOT/SAE J1401` or OEM-equivalent. |
| `BG-KIT-FUEL-001` fuel hoses | Main filler, vent, feed, return, leak-off, breather samples, size references, old clamps, and route labels | Fuel-hose supplier / Land Cruiser parts counter | Must be fuel-rated rubber; main filler hose reference is J40 `77217-60014` NBR. |

## Sample Parts Checklist

### `BG-KIT-HB-001` Rear Handbrake Splitter/Equalizer Cable

Pack these parts or photos:

- Rear splitter/equalizer cable assembly.
- Left rear wheel branch cable with sheath and backing-plate end.
- Right rear wheel branch cable with sheath and backing-plate end.
- Intermediate/front pull cable end or the on-hand long cable for comparison.
- Equalizer/yoke/splitter bar.
- Clevises and threaded clevises.
- Clevis pins.
- R-clips, split pins, and safety clips.
- Washers and spacers.
- Adjuster nut, threaded rod, or barrel adjuster.
- Sheath stops, bracket stops, and grooved ferrules.
- Cable-end retainers.
- Backing-plate lever clips.
- External return/assist springs.
- Cable support clips or saddles.
- Route photos with left/right labels.

### `BG-KIT-BRSPR-001` Rear Brake And Handbrake Springs

Pack these parts or photos:

- Left and right upper shoe return springs.
- Left and right lower shoe return springs.
- Hold-down pins.
- Hold-down cups/retainers.
- Hold-down springs.
- Adjuster/star-wheel springs.
- Adjuster lever springs.
- Parking-brake lever return/assist springs inside the drum.
- External handbrake cable return/assist springs.
- Shoe retaining clips, washers, and E-clips.
- Adjuster screw/star wheel and lever as geometry samples.
- Parking-brake lever clip or hairpin.
- Side labels and opened-drum photos.
- Centric hardware kit contents if on hand, for comparison only until fit is confirmed.

### `BG-KIT-BRHOSE-001` Brake Flex Hoses

Pack these parts or photos:

- Front left brake flex hose complete assembly.
- Front right brake flex hose complete assembly.
- Rear center frame-to-axle brake flex hose complete assembly.
- Chassis-side hose fittings.
- Axle/T-union side fittings.
- Caliper or short-hard-line side fittings.
- Banjo bolts and old copper washers if present, as samples only.
- Retaining U-clips.
- Bracket tabs/grooves or bracket photos.
- Rubber sleeves/grommets if fitted.
- Nearby tee/union sample or photo.
- Route photos at ride height.
- Route photos at axle droop and steering lock.

### `BG-KIT-FUEL-001` Fuel Hoses

Pack these parts or photos:

- Main molded fuel filler hose.
- Small filler-neck vent hose.
- Metal filler neck/cap assembly as a size sample only.
- Tank filler neck/pipe or measured OD gauge.
- Filler vent nipple/pipe sample.
- Tank-to-chassis fuel feed hose sample.
- Chassis-to-filter/lift-pump feed hose sample.
- Fuel return hose sample.
- Injector leak-off/overflow hose sample.
- Tank breather/vent hose sample if separate.
- Rubber grommets/pass-through sleeves on fuel lines if fitted.
- Old clamps for size only.
- Route photos and labels for each hose.

## Safety Corrections

- Brake flex hoses are **brake-rated hydraulic assemblies**, not fuel-rated hose.
- Fuel hoses are **fuel-rated rubber**, not brake hose, coolant hose, vacuum hose, or washer hose.
- Handbrake cables are **control-cable fabrication**, not brake hydraulic hose work.
- Springs are copied by dimensions and tension; do not reuse stretched old springs.
