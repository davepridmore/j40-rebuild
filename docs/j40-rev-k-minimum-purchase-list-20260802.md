# J40 Rev K — simple off-the-shelf / donor purchase list

**Rule:** take the actual original radiator, actual original full-face A/C condenser and existing fan samples to the shop. Measure and test first; buy second.

## Reuse — do not buy again unless the actual item fails

| Qty | Retained item | Shop action |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Original copper/brass engine radiator R0 | Clean, pressure/flow test and refurbish. Preserve its tanks, cap/filler, overflow, hose necks, rails, ears and lower locator geometry. Recore only if earned by condition/test. |
| 1 | Original broad A/C condenser C0, with pipes/drier/tabs | Clean, fin-straighten, flush and pressure-test. Keep it full face if sound; do not replace it with the narrow Rev J render. |
| 1 | Large rear 2H mechanical fan and shroud | Inspect/recondition and seal around R0; confirm blade, hub, runout, engine movement and service clearances. |
| 1 | Existing front electric fan | Remove, photograph, measure and bench-test. Use it only if a true matching mate can be found and both pass. |
| 1 | Expanded-mesh stone guard | Straighten/repair and retain as a removable front item. |
| 1 set | Existing covered relay and MIDI/fuse carrier arrangement | Retain its inboard aligned position, closed covers and service access. |

## Buy only after the full-size mock-up passes

| Qty | Purchase | Plain shop instruction |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 preferred | Matching Toyota/Denso front pusher module | Take the accepted existing fan as a sample. Match the complete frame, diameter, depth, tabs, plug, rotation and current. Prado/Land Cruiser/Toyota breaker stock is a search pool, not a guaranteed direct-fit part number. |
| 2 fallback | Physically matching Toyota/Denso pusher pair | Use only if the old fan cannot be matched or the pair fails fit/airflow/current. Both installed fans must be the same size and centred; keep the old fan as a spare. |
| 1 | Shallow wide CAC/intercooler K0 | One new core only. Start the mock-up around a 450–500 × 150–180 × ≤65 mm **core** target, then buy strictly to the measured complete tanks/ports/coupler envelope. Require pressure-test evidence. |
| 2 sets | Vertical top-locator hardware, size after measurement | One sleeved bolt per original horizontal top-return hole: large washer, EPDM bush, radiator ear, lower washer and locknut. Do not order by the render; measure both holes and the bush stack first. |
| 2 | Toyota-pattern lower radiator rubber saddles | Match the actual R0 lower locators/pins. Both must sit on the load crossmember and share the filled radiator's weight. |
| 1 starting length | 40 × 40 × 3 mm mild-steel SHS for the R0 lower load crossmember | Use only for a measured clear span ≤700 mm and filled R0 mass ≤35 kg; otherwise release a checked larger section. Cut to the measured upright/end-connection geometry. |
| 4 | 4 mm mild-steel triangular crossmember gussets, approximately 50 mm legs | Two at each crossmember/upright junction, or use an equivalently stiff checked removable end-plate connection. |
| As measured | Steel/aluminium carrier material, EPDM seals/isolators, edging, P-clips and fasteners | Separately make the centred twin-fan carrier, low K0 cradle/duct, two short upper R0 restraint ears and only necessary shroud/electrical brackets. None may substitute for the R0 lower load crossmember. |
| As measured | Two fan electrical branches | Relays, covered MIDI/fuses, cable, plugs/pigtails, terminals, earths and sleeving sized from measured start/run current and voltage drop. |

## Buy only if a retained part fails

| Conditional purchase | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| R0 replacement core | R0 fails condition, pressure, flow or instrumented 50 °C duty. Preserve the original external geometry/interfaces. |
| Full-face C0 replacement condenser | C0 fails pressure, flush, fin or fit checks. Replacement must reproduce the measured complete envelope, ports/pipes/drier and mounting interfaces. |
| Rear shroud repair/adapter material | Retained shroud cannot seal R0 or pass fan-sweep/engine-rock clearance. |

## Payment checklist

- Actual R0 and actual full-face C0 are present in the physical fixture.
- Only one new heat exchanger is being bought: K0, for turbo charge air.
- K0, its tanks, ports and couplers fit the measured low-centred envelope without extra side width.
- Two installed front fans are physically matching and symmetric on the vehicle centreline.
- Both original horizontal top holes receive correctly sized sleeved vertical bolts into R0 ears directly beneath; lower rubber saddles carry the weight.
- A continuous full-width lower crossmember visibly connects both structural uprights, with both R0 saddles on it; the filled R0 does not hang from the upper bolts or any other core.
- Exactly three fans are installed: two front electric pushers plus the large rear mechanical puller.
- Covered fuses/relays stay inside the pack silhouette and do not hang from a core.
- No final payment until the opaque full-size fit fixture, installed airflow test and instrumented 50 °C test pass.

> *Purana radiator aur poora A/C condenser saath le jao. Sirf aik naya intercooler khareedo. Do aik-jaisay Toyota/Denso front fan, dono centre mein. Naap aur mock-up pass honay se pehle paisa ya final welding nahin.*
