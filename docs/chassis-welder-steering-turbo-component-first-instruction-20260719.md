# J40 Chassis Welder Instruction — J60 Power Steering and 2H Turbo

Date: 2026-07-19
Vehicle state: Toyota 2H engine installed; body/chassis access currently available
Instruction status: **use this sheet now**

## Instruction to the welder

**Do not fabricate or weld any mounting preparation for the J60 power steering conversion or turbo installation yet. The actual components must be on the vehicle first so their positions can be marked from a trial fit.**

For now:

1. Finish only the already-approved chassis repairs and standard brackets.
2. Clean and inspect the existing steering-box mounting area for cracks, elongated holes, thin metal and previous bad repairs. Photograph it bare. Repair damage only after it is identified and approved; do not alter the mounting pattern for the J60 box yet.
3. Do not add steering-box plates, holes, sleeves, gussets or shaft-clearance cuts until the complete J60 steering assembly is present.
4. Do not add turbo, downpipe, exhaust-hanger, intercooler, heat-shield, airbox or charge-pipe tabs until the chosen turbo/manifold and related component are present.
5. Do not permanently coat over the likely steering-box fabrication zone or the first downpipe/hanger fabrication zone. Apply temporary removable corrosion protection if those areas would otherwise remain bare.
6. Do not remove or loosen the installed 2H engine for either conversion.

## Components required before J60 power-steering fabrication

Bring the following as one matched trial-fit set:

- J60/HJ60 hydraulic steering box;
- matching steering-box chassis mounting plate/bracket, spacers and bolts if available;
- matching pitman arm;
- steering shaft/coupler or the actual spline adapters that will connect the J40 column to the J60 box;
- drag link or confirmed steering-linkage arrangement;
- hydraulic pump with 2H mounting bracket;
- correct crank/pump pulley and belt arrangement;
- reservoir;
- pressure and return hoses with their end fittings.

The steering box, pitman arm, shaft and drag-link geometry must be trial-fitted together before any chassis hole, plate, sleeve or gusset is made. Confirm lock-to-lock travel, axle/spring/shock clearance, steering-joint angles and hose routes first.

## Components required before turbo-related fabrication

For the first hot-side trial fit, bring:

- selected turbocharger with wastegate actuator;
- final 2H turbo manifold or manifold-adapter assembly;
- turbo-to-manifold gasket/studs or exact flange hardware;
- turbine-outlet flange or V-band;
- turbo oil-drain flange;
- rigid template or first section of the proposed downpipe;
- bonnet, inner wing, steering and A/C-compressor envelopes in their installed positions.

Before intercooler or intake-support fabrication, also bring:

- selected intercooler;
- actual turbo outlet and intercooler couplers/reducers;
- selected air-cleaner housing;
- intake-manifold charge-air adaptor/plenum;
- representative charge-pipe bends in the selected diameter.

Only after those parts are bolted or rigidly clamped in their intended positions may the fabricator mark removable hanger, shield, intercooler, airbox or pipe-support brackets.

## Return visit / fabrication sequence

1. Trial-fit the complete J60 steering geometry and mark the required chassis work.
2. Produce a simple cardboard/steel template and record the actual hole centres, plate thickness and sleeve lengths.
3. Obtain approval, then fabricate the steering mount/reinforcement.
4. Trial-fit the selected turbo and manifold on the installed 2H.
5. Establish the downpipe, oil-drain and steering-clearance routes.
6. Trial-fit the intercooler and intake plumbing.
7. Mark only the brackets actually required, obtain approval, then weld them.
