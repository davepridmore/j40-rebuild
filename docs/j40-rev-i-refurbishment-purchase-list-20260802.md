# J40 Rev I — Simple Donor and Workshop Purchase List

**Issue date:** 2 August 2026<br>
**Rule:** take the actual R0 radiator, stone guard and a 1:1 chassis-hole fixture to the shop. Buy the fans and cores only after physical measurement.

## Take to the radiator shop first

- Actual original copper/brass engine radiator **R0**.
- Actual expanded-mesh stone guard.
- Rigid 1:1 template of both existing upper mounting holes and both lower saddle positions.
- Paper measurement sheet for `R0-W/H/D`, `RC-W/H/D`, all necks, filler/cap, overflow, drain, `W0` and `P0`.
- Existing thin aluminium A/C condenser sample for identification, pressure test, flush and measurement.

## Refurbish locally instead of buying

- R0 top and bottom tanks, coolant necks, filler/cap seat, overflow, drain, side rails and mounting ears—retain only if sound; otherwise copy their exact geometry.
- Original stone-guard frame and expanded mesh if straight and at least 70% open.
- Original 2H mechanical fan if hub and blades pass crack, balance and clearance inspection.

## Do not buy until mock-up passes

- Radiator core.
- Charge-air cooler.
- Replacement A/C condenser.
- Rear fan blade or Toyota shroud.
- Steel for the front fan carrier or independent electrical-box brackets.

The released fan positions are fixed: two front hubs at vehicle centreline `C0 − 133 mm` and `C0 + 133 mm`, exactly 266 mm apart. All donor frames, plugs, tabs, guards and wire bends must fit inside the measured opening `W0`.
