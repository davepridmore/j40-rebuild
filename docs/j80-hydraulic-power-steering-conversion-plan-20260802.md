Superseded by: `j60-hydraulic-power-steering-conversion-plan-20260719.md`, informed by the identified fitted J40-type hydraulic baseline

## Historical Decision — Superseded

Use an identified RHD 80-series recirculating-ball steering box and its matched sector/pitman hardware. Connect it to the J40 column through a measured collapsible intermediate shaft, and to the front axle through a measured drag link. Power it with the physically verified 2H pump drive, reservoir and box-specific hoses, matched by measured flow, pressure and relief behavior.

This is not a released welding pattern. The J60 plate/template, pitman arm, shaft coupler and hose assumptions are not transferable without a physical overlay and measurements from the selected J80 parts.

## Controlled Architecture

`J40 column → supported firewall/column lower bearing → collapsible intermediate shaft and two suitable joints → identified RHD J80 box → matched J80 pitman arm → measured/custom drag link → compatible axle steering arm`

`reservoir → H-mounted or independently engineered pump drive with proven pulley alignment/flow/pressure/relief → rated pressure hose with J80-box fitting → J80 box → return hose → optional cooler → reservoir`

The 80-series steering arrangement uses a direct tie-rod and drag-link layout. Do not assume the original J40 relay/centre-arm linkage remains, and do not remove it until the complete new geometry is mocked up and approved.

## Required Matched Components

- identified RHD J80-family steering box with readable complete casting/part identity and known donor application; the observed as-fitted `25050` case mark alone is insufficient;
- matched J80 sector shaft and pitman arm; no mixed J60/J80 pitman parts;
- box mounting bolts, spacers/sleeves and enough donor/frame evidence to map the bolt pattern;
- new J40-to-J80 frame reinforcement/adapter designed from the actual box and truck frame;
- J80-input-spline-compatible lower U-joint/coupler;
- supported J40 column lower end, collapsible intermediate shaft and upper joint;
- measured/custom drag link with compatible joints/tapers at both ends;
- physically verified 2H pump, bracket, crank/water-pump pulley arrangement and belt hardware;
- reservoir with filtered cap where appropriate, suction hose, J80-port-specific pressure hose, return hose and optional cooler;
- hose clamps, heat protection and serviceable routing supports.

## Changes Required Compared With the Previous J60 Plan

1. **Frame mount:** produce a new J40-to-J80 box template and reinforcement. Do not drill from the J60 template.
2. **Column connection:** identify the J80 input spline and select the matching lower joint. Retain a real collapsible section and do not use a solid welded shaft.
3. **Pitman and linkage:** use the pitman arm matched to the J80 sector. Measure the drag-link length at ride height and verify joint/taper compatibility rather than mixing unknown arms or reaming parts by assumption.
4. **Hydraulics:** the J80 box ports and fittings control the pressure/return hose ends. Retain the 2H pump package only after its mounting, flow, pressure, relief behavior, fluid specification and belt alignment are shown compatible with the exact box.
5. **Packaging:** the J80 box, input shaft, pitman sweep, ports, hoses, mount bolts and service-tool path become frozen datums before the battery carrier, turbo manifold/downpipe or charge piping is finalized.

## Battery-Carrier Interaction

The May 17 battery photographs support an approximate DLS120 footprint of `300 x 205 mm`, which fits the `340 x 265 mm` tray in the current orientation. Turning only the battery on that unchanged tray does not fit: `205 x 300 mm` exceeds the tray's `265 mm` short side by `35 mm`. Turning the complete tray/carrier would require an approximately `265 x 340 mm` bay envelope, which the photographs do not measure.

- Keep the current/as-photographed battery-and-tray orientation as the baseline.
- Keep the carrier adjustable at the proposed `160–230 mm` outboard offset range.
- Reserve the lower/inboard chassis-rail corridor for the J80 box, shaft, pitman, hydraulic ports/hoses, mounting bolts and service tools.
- Do not lock the tray height, lateral offset or a whole-carrier rotated orientation until the actual J80 assembly is at least rigidly hard-mocked and the `265 x 340 mm` candidate envelope is measured.

## Trial-Fit Sequence

1. Identify and bench-inspect the J80 box, input spline, sector and matched pitman arm; rebuild before installation if condition is uncertain.
2. Record the bare J40 frame rail and existing steering area. Inspect cracks, elongated holes, previous repairs and rail thickness.
3. Clamp the actual J80 box in the proposed RHD position with a full-size reinforcement template; do not drill or weld.
4. Mock the supported column, collapsible shaft and joints. Check angularity, plunge, engine movement, exhaust/turbo clearance and full steering travel.
5. Fit the matched pitman arm and mock the drag-link line at ride height. Check lock-to-lock travel, bump/rebound, axle articulation, joint angles and chassis/spring/tyre clearance.
6. Mock the pump, brackets, pulley/belt, reservoir and pressure/return hoses. Confirm port access, belt removal and hose movement.
7. Place the `340 x 265 mm` battery tray and `300 x 205 mm` case footprint in the current orientation. Check bonnet, terminal, cable, hold-down and lift-out clearances. Do not rotate only the battery on this tray. Test a `265 x 340 mm` whole-tray rotation only as a separate measured candidate.
8. Mock turbo hot-side, downpipe and heat shields only after steering and battery datums are accepted.
9. Release a dimensioned frame plate, sleeves, gussets, shaft and drag link only from the accepted vehicle mock-up.
10. After fabrication, inspect welds/alignment, flush the hydraulic circuit, bleed with the axle unloaded, check lock-to-lock without binding, and complete low-speed then road-load verification.

## No-Fabrication Holds

- no chassis drilling, welding, sleeves or steering-box plate without the actual identified J80 box;
- no final shaft welding or non-collapsible shaft substitute;
- no pitman/drag-link purchase by model name alone;
- no final pressure hose until both pump and box fittings/specifications are known;
- no final battery-carrier holes, downpipe or turbo heat shield before steering trial fit;
- no road use until steering travel, fastener retention, hydraulic leaks, return-to-centre and bump-steer behavior are verified.
