# Rear Brake Shoe Fitment and Purchase Control — 2026-07-22

## Decision

The received MK `K-2255-N/Y` shoes are **rejected for the project rear axle**. The physical fit-check shows that the shoe is too wide and does not seat correctly at the fitted backing-plate/wheel-cylinder arrangement. Do not grind, trim, bend, reline or install it.

The controlled replacement target is:

- MK Kashiyama / MK Yellow `K-2221` or `K-2221-N/Y`
- Toyota `04494-60010` or `04494-60011`
- One complete rear-axle set: confirm **four shoes**, not one loose shoe or one wheel pair
- Working width target about `55–57 mm`, subject to direct old-sample and backing-plate verification

The manufacturer’s 2024–2025 application catalogue assigns `K2221` to the rear of the `BJ40/FJ40/FJ55/HJ45` family through July 1980 in the general-market table. In the same table, `K2255` appears in the front column for the front-drum model from September 1975. From August 1980, the catalogue changes the rear route to `K2280`. See the [official MK Kashiyama catalogue](https://www.mk-kashiyama.com/mkkwp/wp-content/uploads/Book-Catalog-2024_fh.pdf), especially the Land Cruiser general/Asia-market table on PDF page 437.

Because this J40 has a modified/restored history, catalogue year alone is not an installation release. The old known-fit shoe, actual drum and fitted backing plate remain the final authority.

## Acceptance checklist for K-2221

Before payment or dispatch:

- Box and shoe marking read `K-2221` or `K-2221-N/Y`.
- Supplier confirms the package contains four shoes for one rear axle.
- Seller ruler/caliper photo shows approximately `55–57 mm` shoe/lining width and matches the old sample.
- Overall arc/diameter is compared with the old shoe and the measured drum ID.
- Shoe web ends match the wheel-cylinder slots and lower anchor/adjuster.
- Hold-down holes, spring holes and parking-brake lever/clip location match the old layout.

At the workshop:

- Dry-fit one side without forcing any part and rotate the drum by hand.
- Confirm full shoe seating, adjuster range, parking-brake application and complete free return.
- Repeat on the other side; keep primary/secondary shoe orientation and LH/RH hardware controlled.
- After hydraulic assembly, adjust and bleed; carry out a controlled low-speed stop, drag/temperature comparison and leak reinspection before public-road use.

## Hard stops

- No K-2255 grinding, lining trimming or web modification.
- No substitution to `K-2280`, `K-2255` or an unmarked local shoe without a physical sample and dimension match.
- No installation based on seller vehicle-title text alone.
- No public-road brake test until the drums, shoes, wheel cylinders, springs, adjusters, parking brake and hydraulics all pass assembly and functional checks.
