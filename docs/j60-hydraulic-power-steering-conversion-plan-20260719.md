# J40 to J60 Hydraulic Power-Steering Conversion Plan

> **Reactivated 2026-08-02.** The five-view photo intake identifies the fitted unit as a later J40-family factory-type hydraulic gear; the owner confirms the RHD J60/HJ60 replacement remains the planned upgrade. The intervening J80 route is superseded. Keep this J60 route component-first: do not fabricate from assumed mounting, shaft, pitman or linkage dimensions.

Date: 2026-07-19
Vehicle: right-hand-drive J40, Toyota 2H installed
Status: active steering-conversion route; component acquisition and trial fit required before fabrication

## Decision

Replace the J40's fitted later-40-Series factory-type hydraulic steering gear with a **right-hand-drive 60-series Land Cruiser steering box**, a 2H-compatible hydraulic pump drive, and a component-in-hand chassis mounting conversion.

This supersedes the Yaris/Vitz column-EPS route. Do not buy or fabricate EPS parts unless this decision is explicitly reopened.

The conversion is not released as a dimensioned welding job yet. The exact box, pitman arm, steering shaft, drag link and pump drive must be obtained first and trial-fitted on the truck. Commercial FJ40/FJ60 conversion systems use a dedicated frame plate, mounting template/hardware, telescoping shaft/joints, pump, hoses, pulley and pitman/drag-link components; that confirms the system must be treated as one coordinated package rather than a loose steering-box swap.

## Target Architecture

`J40 steering wheel/column → collapsible intermediate shaft and U-joints → RHD J60/HJ60 power-steering box → matched pitman arm → custom/measured drag link → front axle steering arm`

Hydraulic circuit:

`reservoir → 2H-mounted pump → rated high-pressure hose → J60 steering box → return hose → optional cooler → reservoir`

The J60 box conversion should replace the fitted J40-type hydraulic gear and any baseline linkage parts that are incompatible with the approved J60 geometry. Do not discard, retain in service or modify the current pitman/linkage components until the complete new linkage is physically laid out and approved.

## Stage 1 — Confirm the Truck Before Buying

Record:

- chassis/model plate and build year;
- right-hand-drive steering-box side and current column exit;
- installed 2H engine number and front accessory/pulley arrangement;
- front axle type, steering-arm/tie-rod-end taper and current drag-link connection;
- sprung-under/sprung-over configuration, final Ironman ride height and caster plan;
- tyre diameter and wheel offset;
- radiator support, inner-wing, shock, spring, brake-line and engine-mount clearances;
- condition of the existing steering-box rail, including cracks, elongated holes, thinning and previous repairs.

Output: labelled photographs and measurements. No conversion holes or plates at this stage.

## Stage 2 — Acquire One Matched Donor/Conversion Set

### Mandatory steering components

- one identified **RHD J60/HJ60-family hydraulic steering box**, preferably inspected/rebuilt before installation;
- readable donor/model or part-number identification;
- matching pitman arm, sector-shaft nut and washer;
- FJ40-to-J60 conversion frame plate/template or enough donor mounting hardware to design a measured equivalent;
- all frame bolts, sleeves/crush tubes and outer spacers as a controlled set;
- collapsible/telescoping intermediate steering shaft;
- correct U-joints/couplers for both the J40 column and selected J60-box input splines;
- drag link with confirmed steering-box-end and axle-end joint/taper compatibility;
- steering-column support bearing if the original lower support is removed by the conversion.

### Mandatory hydraulic components

- power-steering pump compatible with the selected J60 box;
- **2H-specific pump mounting bracket** and adjustment hardware;
- crank/pump pulley arrangement matching the 2H belt section and alignment;
- reservoir, cap and mounting bracket;
- new pressure-rated high-pressure hose with fittings matching both pump and box;
- correct oil-resistant low-pressure return hose and clamps/fittings;
- selected power-steering fluid specification documented from the box/pump service basis;
- optional return-line cooler sized and mounted only after route/airflow review.

### Buy/no-buy gate

Do not buy a loose box unless the seller also provides or proves:

- RHD orientation;
- input and sector splines;
- mounting pattern and port locations;
- pitman-arm match;
- rebuild/leak/play condition;
- a credible path to the frame plate, sleeves, shaft joints, pump bracket and hoses.

Do not mix an unidentified high-output pump with the J60 box. Pump flow/pressure and relief behavior must be compatible with the box.

## Stage 3 — Bench Inspection Before Fabrication

1. Clean the donor box without forcing contamination into seals/ports.
2. Check sector-shaft and input-shaft play, roughness, binding, casing cracks, damaged threads and evidence of leakage.
3. Centre the box by counting total input turns and setting the midpoint; verify the pitman-arm indexing/master spline.
4. Inspect or rebuild the box before chassis fabrication if condition is uncertain.
5. Inspect the pump shaft, bearing, pulley groove and bracket for play, cracks and misalignment.
6. Flush or replace used reservoir and hard fittings; use new hoses for final installation.
7. Record every spline, thread, hose port, bolt diameter and mounting-hole centre from the actual parts.

Gate: no chassis template is released until the box and linkage components pass inspection.

## Stage 4 — Full Vehicle Trial Fit

Trial-fit on the installed 2H and assembled front running gear:

1. Set the front axle at final ride height and planned caster.
2. Centre the road wheels and steering box independently.
3. Clamp the J60 box and cardboard/steel frame-plate template in the proposed RHD position.
4. Install the pitman arm and mock the full drag link.
5. Mock the telescoping shaft from the J40 column to the box with correct U-joint phasing and spline engagement.
6. Cycle steering from lock to lock while checking the tyre, spring, shock, chassis, radiator support, engine mount, brake lines and future turbo/downpipe corridor.
7. Cycle the axle through bump and droop with springs/shocks disconnected or by an approved safe articulation method.
8. Confirm that the pitman/drag-link arrangement does not over-travel the axle stops or create binding/bump steer.
9. Trial-fit pump, bracket, pulley, belt, reservoir and hose routes on the 2H.
10. Confirm service access to belts, pump adjustment, reservoir, steering-box adjuster/ports and all mounting bolts.

Gate: owner/workshop approves labelled mock-up photographs before drilling or welding.

## Stage 5 — Produce the Actual Fabrication Card

The fabricator now transfers dimensions from the trial fit and records:

- box mounting-hole centres and bolt diameters;
- inner reinforcement plate outline/thickness;
- any outer spacer/plate outline and thickness;
- sleeve/crush-tube OD, ID and exact length for each through-bolt;
- plate-to-rail contact and weld/bolt arrangement;
- shaft clearance opening, if required;
- pitman-arm and drag-link clearance envelopes;
- pump-bracket and reservoir mounting interfaces;
- brake/fuel-line relocation tabs required by the final box position.

Use an established FJ40/J60 conversion plate/template where obtainable. If locally reproducing it, the physical box and trial-fit template control every dimension. No dimensions are to be taken from internet photographs.

## Stage 6 — Chassis Fabrication

1. Remove the trial-fitted steering box, hydraulic parts and vulnerable components from the weld area.
2. Strip the approved rail zone to sound bare metal and crack-check it.
3. Repair existing cracks/thinning separately before adding conversion reinforcement.
4. Drill only from the approved physical template.
5. Fit structural sleeves/crush tubes so mounting bolts cannot collapse the boxed rail.
6. Fit the approved inner reinforcement plate and any required outer spacer/plate.
7. Tack with the box/template bolted in position; recheck shaft, pitman and linkage geometry.
8. Remove the box before final welding.
9. Complete controlled welds, inspect, deburr and restore corrosion protection only after dry-fit sign-off.
10. Reinstall using new graded hardware and the torque specification supplied for the selected box/conversion kit.

No steering-box load may be carried by an unsupported single chassis skin.

## Stage 7 — Hydraulic and Mechanical Installation

- Install the centred steering box and centred wheel/column without moving either off centre.
- Fit the collapsible shaft with full spline engagement, correct U-joint phasing and positive pinch-bolt retention.
- Fit the pitman arm at its indexed centre position.
- Fabricate/adjust the drag link to hold the road wheels straight with the box centred.
- Install the 2H pump bracket and verify pulley alignment with a straightedge.
- Fit a belt of the correct section and adjustment range.
- Mount the reservoir upright, accessible and away from exhaust/turbo heat.
- Route the pressure hose without twist, chafe or exhaust contact and with movement allowance.
- Route return hose through an optional cooler only if the cooler is securely mounted and does not restrict return flow.
- Keep all hydraulic hoses clear of the future turbo manifold/downpipe zone.

## Stage 8 — Fill, Bleed and Static Acceptance

Follow the selected box/pump service procedure and fluid specification.

Acceptance checks:

- no leaks at rest or under assist;
- pump quiet after air removal;
- fluid does not foam or overflow;
- steering moves smoothly through full travel with front wheels unloaded, then loaded;
- steering stops are reached before the box or linkage internally binds;
- equal usable travel left and right with the box centred;
- no hose stretch, twist, rubbing or contact at either lock;
- no shaft/U-joint binding, excessive angle, looseness or contact;
- all structural fasteners torqued and paint-marked;
- steering wheel, box, pitman arm and road wheels remain mutually centred.

## Stage 9 — Alignment and Road Validation

1. Complete caster/toe/alignment after final suspension ride height is established.
2. Verify steering-wheel return-to-centre, effort and straight tracking at walking speed.
3. Increase speed progressively on a controlled route.
4. Check bump steer, wander, shimmy, pump noise, heat and leaks after each stage.
5. Reinspect frame plate, sleeves, bolts, pitman arm, drag link, shaft joints, pump bracket, belt and hoses after the first short drive and again after the workshop-defined bedding interval.
6. Stop use immediately for cracking, fastener movement, fluid loss, binding, poor return, unexpected toe change or contact.

## Coordination With the Turbo Conversion

The J60 steering box, shaft, pump, reservoir, belt and hoses are installed/mocked **before** the turbo hot-side position is finalized. The turbo manifold and downpipe must then be positioned around the proven steering system—not vice versa.

No steering hose may run within an unshielded turbo/manifold/downpipe heat zone. Turbo heat shields and hose protection are designed after both systems are physically present.
