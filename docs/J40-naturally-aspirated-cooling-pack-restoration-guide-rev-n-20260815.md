# J40 Naturally Aspirated Cooling + A/C Restoration Guide — Rev N

**Issue:** 15 August 2026
**Status:** controlled shop guide; measured drawings, structural fabrication release and installed performance release remain **HOLD**
**Applies to:** R0 copper/brass radiator, C0 full-face A/C condenser, G0 stone guard, one FS front A/C pusher, FL rear radiator puller/shroud, existing chassis support structure, new compact mounting parts, hoses, fittings, wiring and finishes
**Decision:** naturally aspirated Toyota 2H; no turbocharger, no intercooler/K0, no charge-air pipework and no second small front fan

Rev N supersedes Rev M for the cooling-pack mounting design. It adds the compact removable X2-L/X2-R end-attachment pair and the complete fabricated mounting-parts schedule. Rev M and Rev L remain historical evidence only. Photographs and generated images are not fabrication drawings.

## 1. Released architecture

The controlled air path is:

**grille → G0 removable stone guard → one centred FS A/C pusher → full-face C0 condenser → R0 radiator → sealed FL rear puller/shroud → engine**

- FS serves condenser airflow during A/C operation. FL remains the radiator fan.
- C0, FS, FL and G0 use independent removable supports; no heat exchanger hangs from another core.
- R0 is carried only by its two lower locators in two new rubber saddles. Its upper fittings locate and restrain without supporting weight.
- One front fan remains the controlled design unless logged hot-idle A/C testing justifies a future revision.

## 2. Rev N compact chassis-attachment decision

The corrected lower load path is:

**R0 lower locators → R1 rubber saddles → X1 formed seats → X0 lower crossmember → X2-L/X2-R compact removable end brackets → accepted existing left/right structural pickups**

The new X2 brackets must finish close to the released fastener and load-transfer envelope. They must not extend into unused tall plates or long projecting arms merely for appearance or convenience. Their exact footprint still has to include the calculated bearing area, fastener edge distance, gusset/weld effective length, tool sweep, removal path, drainage and coating access.

The July-welded uprights are retained chassis structure, not trim-to-fit mounting blanks. Do not cut, shorten, notch, drill, slot, ream or grind an upright, its parent load path, any weld toe or heat-affected zone as a packaging adjustment. A pre-existing projection may be trimmed only if the U0 measured template and written competent-person release identify it as non-structural surplus and control the cut line, remaining ligament, edge distance, weld/HAZ exclusion, closure, drainage, repair and corrosion protection. Otherwise stop and redesign the removable bracket.

If the existing structure cannot provide one U0-released connection interface per side, do not improvise a side bolt or local weld. Recalculate and issue a later controlled structural design.

## 3. Non-negotiable measured holds

Do not buy final hoses, drill final brackets, weld the cradle or coat any fabrication until all of these are recorded from the actual vehicle and retained parts:

1. **B0:** rigid template of both original upper chassis holes, including diameter and XYZ centres.
2. **S0:** rigid template of both R0 lower locators, saddle centres, locator diameters and installed rubber height.
3. **U0-L/U0-R:** rigid mirror-handed templates of the left/right lower-crossmember attachment interfaces: released existing pickup holes/faces and XYZ, local parent section and condition, attachment plane, weld/HAZ exclusion, obstacles, drainage, tool sweep and removal path.
4. **L0/MR:** clear X0 span including end-connection geometry, plus filled/capped radiator mass.
5. **R0/C0 maps:** full external envelopes, tabs, ports, pipes, cap, drain, necks, ears, locators and service-tool sweeps.
6. **FS/FL maps:** complete frames, tabs, guards, motors, plugs and cable bends—not blade/ring diameter alone.
7. **Vehicle envelope:** grille, bumper, bonnet/latch, steering, suspension, tow structure, support steel, hoses, A/C lines, engine movement, wiring and tool access.
8. **Opaque 1:1 dry fit:** actual parts and realistic rubbers, fasteners, hoses, wiring and tools pass before final cutting, drilling or coating.

## 4. Step-by-step shop sequence

### Step 1 — Identify, photograph and baseline-test

- Tag R0, C0, G0, FS and FL; mark front/rear, left/right and vehicle centreline.
- Photograph every face, label, port, plug, solder joint, crack and mounting point with a scale.
- Cap open coolant and refrigerant ports. Keep C0 dry internally.
- Record R0 pressure/flow, C0 specialist leak/contamination condition and FS/FL terminal voltage, start current, run current, rotation and airflow direction.

**Gate:** signed retain/repair/replace decisions before cosmetic stripping.

### Step 2 — Capture B0, S0 and the new U0 interfaces

- Make rigid B0, S0 and U0-L/U0-R templates that cannot rack.
- Record L0, MR and every component/vehicle envelope on one dated, toleranced drawing from the vehicle centreline and fixed chassis datums.
- Mark the existing upright material, wall/plate geometry, every weld toe/HAZ and any damage before proposing X2.
- Build an opaque full-size pack dummy and prove bonnet/latch, grille, cap, drain, hoses, A/C unions, plugs, wiring and tool access.

**Gate:** no photograph, old nominal dimension or render substitutes for the measured release drawing.

### Step 3 — Recore and restore R0

1. Baseline pressure- and flow-test; inspect tanks, headers, necks, cap seat, overflow, drain, rails, ears and locators for thinning, cracks, dezincification and failed solder.
2. Jig the retained geometry before heat. Budget for a new naturally aspirated-duty core because the photographed core/fin pack is tired.
3. Remove the historical added radiator support leg after recording it; do not reproduce it.
4. Unsolder the tanks/headers as required, remove the old core, clean and tin sound retained brass/copper, fit the selected replacement core squarely, and resolder without moving the neck, drain, locator or sealing-face geometry.
5. Use inhibited radiator-shop chemistry compatible with copper, brass and the actual solder. No abrasive blast, uncontrolled pressure washing, DIY strong acid or caustic.
6. Repeat pressure and flow tests after all hot work. Mask openings, cap seat, threads, hose lands, labels and rubber contacts; apply only a thin radiator heat-exchanger coating.

**Gate:** square, pressure-tight, free-flowing, clean solder, open straight fins and written test results.

### Step 4 — Restore C0 through an automotive A/C shop

- Identify refrigerant, oil and seal system before opening.
- Clean with neutral aluminium-safe coil cleaner, a low-pressure clean-side rinse and careful fin combing in tube direction.
- Flush only by an approved closed procedure. Leak-test with dry nitrogen or forming gas at the applicable service pressure; never oxygen or wet shop air.
- Preserve the full face, pipes, brackets and tool sweeps. Install a new compatible receiver-drier, HNBR seals/O-rings and caps at final assembly; the old drier is a geometry sample only.

**Gate:** capped clean ports, straight fins and a written specialist leak result.

### Step 5 — Restore G0 and qualify FS/FL

- Jig and repair the guard; preserve mesh pitch/open area and drainage. Finish in thin satin/semi-gloss black without bridging apertures.
- Retain exactly one complete FS module only after confirming it pushes grille-to-engine in its designed orientation and passes frame, blade, motor, current and airflow checks.
- Retain FL only after confirming it pulls engineward, its complete shroud is sound and blade runout, bearings, current, plug and installed airflow pass.
- Clean fans with plastic-safe detergent. Do not paint blades, motors, vents, labels, plugs or rubbers.

**Gate:** documented safe current/direction, no crack/wobble/rub and credible installed airflow.

### Step 6 — Design X0/X1/X2 around measured attachments

- X0 is the lower load-carrying crossmember. **40 × 40 × 3 mm mild-steel SHS is packaging/prototype starting stock only when L0 ≤ 700 mm and MR ≤ 35 kg.** It is never an automatic structural release.
- A competent automotive structural engineer or suitably qualified fabricator must accept vertical, fore/aft and lateral road loads, shock, vibration, fatigue, X0 section, X1 seats, X2 end connections, parent interfaces, welds, fasteners and torque.
- Form two level X1 seats at S0. Each R0 locator must fully seat in a new R1 rubber saddle without tank, seam or metal contact.
- Design X2-L/X2-R as compact mirror-handed removable/gusseted parts using only U0-released existing structural faces/holes. Never attach them to steering, suspension, bumper, tow or thin/non-structural sheet. Minimise unused projection while preserving the released structural and service envelope.
- Do not add chassis holes, slots, reaming, forced bolt pull, loose washer-stack spacers or an unsleeved rubber joint.

**Gate:** released drawing and structural record identify every X0/X1/X2 outline, material, bend, hole, gusset, weld, fastener, torque and interface.

### Step 7 — Make T0 and the independent carriers

- Set R0 naturally on both lower saddles, then transfer B0 into two short removable T0 locator ears from the released drawing.
- Use a new EPDM bush, fitted steel crush sleeve, broad washer, zinc class 8.8 bolt and locking nut at each original hole. Sleeve length controls rubber compression.
- T0 must remain neutral when tightened; it must not lift, pull or unload either saddle.
- Make independent removable C1 condenser brackets, F1 single-pusher carrier, F2 rear-shroud supports, G1 guard stand-offs and E1 relay/fuse plate.
- Isolate C0 and released fan/guard connections with appropriate EPDM and shoulder/crush sleeves. Seal FL to the R0 frame with closed-cell EPDM without obscuring fins, drainage, cap, drain or fasteners.

**Gate:** every fabricated component is tagged to the schedule and independently removable without core load.

### Step 8 — Complete the bare-metal 1:1 dry fit

- Assemble grille → G0 → one FS → C0 → R0 → FL → engine using the actual rubbers, brackets, plugs, cable bends, hoses, A/C pipes and service tools.
- Confirm the two lower saddles share load and T0 remains neutral. Confirm X2 can be removed with the accepted upright untouched.
- Prove bonnet/latch and engine movement, plus removal/service paths for G0, FS, C0, R0, FL, cap, drain, drier, unions, plugs and fasteners.
- Photograph every released gap and freeze the drawings only after sign-off.

**Gate:** fabricator and owner sign the bare-metal fit drawing before coating.

### Step 9 — Structural proof, prepare and coat

- After structural release, apply a **total static proof load of 2 × MR**, distributed between the two S0 saddle centres in the released loaded-radiator distribution, for **10 minutes**. The structural release must define that distribution and a fixture that prevents local point-loading of the rubber or crossmember. This supplements—not replaces—road-load/fatigue calculation.
- Fail for permanent set, crack, fastener movement, saddle displacement, X2/X0 movement or upright distortion.
- Deburr and radius exposed edges; remove spatter without grinding structural welds flush.
- Degrease, mechanically clean and use phosphoric rust treatment only on isolated ferrous steel where required.
- Apply one compatible 2K epoxy-primer/2K polyurethane satin or semi-gloss system. Mask threads, earths, drains, rubber seats and mating faces. Cavity-wax eligible closed steel only after full cure.

**Gate:** proof passes before paint; finished parts have no rust, blocked drain, coated earth or overspray on a core/rubber interface.

### Step 10 — Install coolant and A/C service parts

- Buy new moulded upper/lower/overflow hoses only after the final neck map. Fit constant-tension clamps behind the hose beads.
- Fit the approved cap, Toyota 2H thermostat/seal and one compatible long-life coolant family mixed with deionised water as specified.
- Keep the new receiver-drier sealed until connection. Lubricate new HNBR seals only with the identified compatible refrigerant oil, then evacuate, leak-check and charge to the actual system specification.

**Gate:** cold coolant/A/C leak checks pass and every fluid/service part is recorded.

### Step 11 — Wire two independent fan branches

- FS receives its own sealed relay, covered fuse, measured-current cable and earth, controlled from A/C demand through appropriate high-side-pressure logic.
- FL receives a separate sealed relay, fuse, cable and earth under coolant-temperature control.
- Use sealed connectors, proper crimps, adhesive heat-shrink, abrasion loom, grommets and rubber-lined P-clips. Route exits rearward/downward with drip control.

**Gate:** record terminal voltage, voltage drop, start/run current, charging voltage and fuse/relay/cable temperature.

### Step 12 — Commission and release

- Bleed coolant, hand-check fan clearance, test each fan independently and inspect for leaks, rub, vibration and mount movement through a full heat cycle.
- At hot idle in the intended ambient duty, log A/C pressures/temperatures and FS state against the actual refrigerant/system specification.
- Road/load-log naturally aspirated coolant temperature, speed/load and FL state. Fix direction, voltage drop, sealing, bypass and distribution before considering another fan.
- Reinspect X0/X1/X2/T0 witness marks, rubbers, leaks, wiring and hoses after cool-down and the first loaded journey.

**Gate:** no boil, purge, progressive coolant rise, progressive A/C high-side escalation or witness-mark movement.

Use weld consumables matched to the actual parent steel and approved procedure. Follow every SDS. 2K coatings can contain hazardous isocyanates and may require professional spray controls and supplied-air respiratory protection. Never weld near chlorinated-cleaner residue, pressure-test A/C with oxygen or vent refrigerant.

## 8. Release record

1. **Component integrity:** R0 pressure/flow, C0 leak/cleanliness and FS/FL electrical/direction tests pass.
2. **Geometry:** B0, S0, U0, L0, MR and all component/vehicle/tool/movement envelopes are on the dated drawing.
3. **Structure:** existing uprights/weld/HAZ, X0/X1/X2/T0 and every connection pass competent-person structural release.
4. **Proof:** a total static 2 × MR, distributed between the two S0 centres per the released loaded-radiator distribution, passes for 10 minutes before coating.
5. **Dry fit:** natural lower seating, neutral upper locators, independent supports and every service/removal path pass.
6. **Finish/assembly:** new rubbers/service parts, correct fluids, matching hardware, clear drains and protected earths pass.
7. **Installed performance:** independent fan circuits, hot-idle A/C and naturally aspirated road/load cooling remain stable.
