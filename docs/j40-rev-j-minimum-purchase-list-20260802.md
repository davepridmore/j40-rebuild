# J40 Rev J — simple donor-parts purchase list

**Issue:** 2 August 2026<br>
**Rule:** take the existing parts to the shop first. Measure, test and sample-match before paying.

## Reuse these; do not buy them again

| Qty | Existing item | Shop action |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Actual wide copper/brass engine radiator R0 | Clean, inspect, pressure/flow test and refurbish. Keep tanks, cap/filler, overflow, necks, side rails, ears and lower locator geometry if sound. Recore only after a failed inspection/test or failed 50 °C duty. |
| 1 | Large rear 2H mechanical fan and shroud | Inspect/recondition; repair/seal the shroud only as needed. |
| 1 | Existing front electric fan | Photograph, measure and bench-test. Use as the sample for a matching twin if accepted. |
| 1 | Existing A/C condenser | Identify, pressure-test, flush and fit-check; retain if passed. |
| 1 | Removable expanded-mesh stone guard | Straighten/repair and retain. |
| 1 set | Existing covered relay and MIDI/fuse carrier arrangement | Retain its alignment inside the cooling-pack width; inspect covers, terminals and service access. |

## Buy after the parts are measured

| Qty | Buy | What to ask for |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 preferred | Matching Toyota/Denso-family complete front fan module | Take the existing accepted front fan as the sample. Match complete outside size, blade diameter, motor/frame/guard, mounting tabs, pusher rotation, plug and current. Buy with plug and useful pigtail. Prado/Land Cruiser/Toyota breaker stock is a practical search pool, not a guaranteed direct-fit part number. |
| 2 only if required | Physically matched Toyota/Denso-family complete fan pair | Fallback only if the existing fan cannot be matched or the proposed pair fails fit, current, installed ≥3,000 m³/h airflow or 50 °C test. Both units must be identical/equal envelope; keep the old fan as a spare. |
| 1 | Compact CAC/intercooler | One only. Buy after `W0/P0` and pipe route prove it can share one side-by-side plane with the condenser. Require tanks/ports, brackets and pressure-test evidence. Target route pressure drop ≤10 kPa. |
| As measured | Split auxiliary carrier, centred fan carrier, divider, seals, EPDM isolators, edging and P-clips | Simple removable local fabrication based on the 1:1 fixture. No side annex and no new chassis holes. |
| 2 assemblies | M8 top-locator hardware | Two bolts total: one zinc-plated vertical M8 bolt through each original horizontal top-return hole, with a large washer, sleeve, EPDM/rubber bush and locking nut into the short R0 ear below. |
| 2 | Lower radiator rubber saddles/isolators | Match the actual R0 lower rails/locators; these carry radiator weight. |
| As measured | Fan electrical service parts | Two independently protected branches: relays, MIDI/fuses, cable, terminals/connectors, earths and sleeving sized from measured start/run current. Reuse the existing covered carrier alignment. |

## Buy only if inspection rejects the retained item

| Conditional item | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| R0 replacement core | Existing core fails condition/pressure/flow checks or instrumented 50 °C duty. Preserve R0 external geometry. |
| A/C condenser | Existing condenser fails pressure, flush, fin, port or fit checks. Replacement must fit its released half of the shallow auxiliary plane. |
| Rear shroud repair/adapter material | Retained shroud cannot seal R0 or pass blade-sweep/engine-rock clearance. |
| R0 tank/neck/ear repair piece | Original is demonstrably unsafe; copy its geometry before removal. |

## Payment checklist

- Two installed front fan modules are physically matching, equal-size pushers; complete group is symmetric about `C0`.
- `F`, `g`, `Wc` and `M = (Wc − 2F − g) / 2` have been recorded; both margins are positive and equal.
- CAC and condenser fit **side-by-side in one shallow plane**, each behind one fan; they are not stacked one behind the other.
- R0 remains the only full-width engine-coolant radiator behind both auxiliary zones.
- Both original horizontal top holes have a 1:1 template and will receive vertical M8 bolts into R0 ears directly below; lower saddles carry the weight.
- Exactly three installed fans: two matching electric pushers plus the retained large rear mechanical puller.
- Final release remains subject to mock-up, ≥3,000 m³/h installed front-pair airflow and instrumented 50 °C testing.
