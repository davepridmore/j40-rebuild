# Yaris-First EPS Conversion Fabrication Plan - General Adapter Route

Date: 2026-05-22
Scope: planning and fabrication controls for fitting a column-assist EPS unit into the J40 through a new General EPS Adapter. The active sourcing plan is now **Yaris-first** for Pakistan availability: first scout local Pakistan Yaris sedan EPS donor/installer packages, then older Yaris/Belta XP90-family sets, with Vitz XP90 as fallback if a complete matched set appears. The adapter remains the controlling vehicle-side interface.

## Core Decision

This is a column-assist EPS graft. It is not a hydraulic steering conversion and does not require power-steering pumps, hydraulic boxes, hoses, crank pulleys, or engine removal.

Preferred route: retain as much of the J40 upper-column driving position, wheel location, switch/stalk relationship, and dash ergonomics as practical. Package a Yaris-family EPS assist unit lower in the column path using a fabricated, removable General EPS Adapter rather than assuming the donor column brackets will bolt directly to the J40.

The General EPS Adapter is a mechanical interface only. It does not replace the matched EPS ECU/controller, donor pigtails, shaft hardware, U-joints, couplers, bench-test requirement, or steering validation. Do not buy or install a bare EPS column.

Local Yaris sedan donor note: current Pakistan Yaris availability makes this the first market-scout target, but it is higher-risk until the exact EPS layout is physically proven. Accept it only if the assist unit, controller/module, torque sensor, connectors, shafts/couplers, and bench operation can be demonstrated outside the donor car. Reject or keep quote-only if it needs donor body ECU/immobilizer/VSC/CAN messages, scan-tool calibration, or a rack-assist-only layout with no safe adapter path.

## General EPS Adapter Definition

The adapter is a removable bolt-in fabrication package that standardizes the J40 side of the conversion and isolates donor-specific variation to replaceable plates/couplers.

It consists of:

- J40 vehicle-side datum plate tied into the lower dash/cowl/firewall structure.
- Firewall doubler/spreader plate around the column pass-through where sheet metal receives load.
- EPS body saddle or clamp cradle that carries motor torque reaction into the adapter, not into thin dash sheet alone.
- Slotted clocking/spacing plates for final column angle, motor rotation, and wheel-position adjustment after mock-up.
- Replaceable upper and lower shaft interface pieces so spline/DD/coupler changes do not require remaking the whole support.
- Braced controller carrier or nearby mounting face if the EPS ECU/controller location is part of the same service area.
- Service access for adapter fasteners, coupler pinch bolts, controller connectors, fuse/ground labels, and later column removal.

Adapter rule: donor brackets, firewall plates, and support plates are useful reference pieces, but the final J40 installation is released from the General EPS Adapter mock-up, not from donor bracket geometry alone.

## 1) Layout And Measurement

Measure and photograph before cutting:

- J40 steering-wheel height and reach.
- Existing upper-column angle.
- Dash hole and lower dash support position.
- Pedal clearance through full pedal travel.
- Firewall pass-through center and available reinforcement area.
- Steering-box input shaft position and angle.
- Donor EPS motor/gearbox envelope.
- Donor input/output shaft length, spline or DD form, and bracket spacing.
- Proposed EPS ECU/controller mounting envelope.
- General adapter datum points: lower dash/cowl pickup positions, firewall doubler footprint, pedal box clearance, steering-box input centerline, and service-tool access.
- Adapter stack height and spacer allowance between J40 structure, EPS body cradle, and shaft centerline.

Hold point: no irreversible column cutting, shaft cutting, drilling, welding, or firewall enlargement until the General EPS Adapter centerline, motor envelope, clocking, shaft path, pedal/knee clearance, and service access are mocked up and photographed.

## 2) General Adapter And Column Support Fabrication

The EPS motor creates torque reaction. The support must carry that load through strong structure, not thin dash sheet alone.

Fabricate the adapter as a controlled subassembly:

- Vehicle-side datum plate that lands on proven J40 structure.
- Triangulated under-dash/cowl/firewall cradle tied to the datum plate.
- EPS body saddle or split clamp around the donor EPS housing where the casting/bracket shape permits.
- Clocking plate or slotted shim pack for final motor rotation and column angle.
- Firewall/cowl doubler or spreader plate where sheet metal receives load.
- Replaceable donor-side interface plate if a later EPS unit has a different bracket pattern.
- Bolt-on controller carrier if the ECU/controller is mounted near the adapter.
- Serviceable fastener access for later column, adapter, or controller removal.
- Clearance cutouts only where they do not weaken steering support.

Material starting point:

- 3 mm mild steel bracket stock or equivalent.
- 4 mm mild steel for firewall doubler, datum plates, high-load tabs, or gussets where the mock-up proves load concentration.
- 40 x 40 x 4 mm angle/L-section where an under-dash cradle or triangulated adapter leg benefits from pre-formed stock.
- Tube or sleeve spacers only after final M8/M10 through-bolt size and gap are confirmed.
- M8/M10 class 8.8 fasteners where structural fastening is needed.
- Welded tabs only after full dry-fit, if bolted support alone is not adequate and the weld location is clear of wiring, seals, and finished coating.

Fabrication release sequence:

1. Cardboard or thin-sheet template for datum plate, firewall doubler, and EPS saddle.
2. Flat-bar or first-metal mock-up bolted in place with the donor EPS unit installed.
3. Wheel position, motor clocking, shaft centerline, pedal travel, knee clearance, heater/dash service, and wiring route photos.
4. Adapter fastener map with bolt size, sleeve/spacer need, access side, and torque/paint-mark plan.
5. Final adapter fabrication only after the mock-up steers manually lock-to-lock without bind.

Reject design if the motor can rock the column, if support depends on one thin sheet-metal flange, if slots are used as a substitute for structure after final alignment, or if the bracket blocks pedals, knees, heater/dash service, wiring, controller access, or column removal.

## 3) Shaft, U-Joint And Coupler Adaptation

The steering shaft path is safety-critical.

Required controls:

- Lay out all donor shaft sections, J40 shaft ends, U-joints, couplers, and pinch bolts together.
- Identify spline count/diameter or DD dimensions before machining.
- Treat the General EPS Adapter as the shaft-interface datum; upper and lower coupler choices must match the adapter centerline, not just the donor column length.
- Keep adequate shaft engagement at every coupler.
- Phase U-joints correctly.
- Check angles lock-to-lock before final fastening.
- Use machined adapters or correct splined/DD couplers.
- Use pinch bolts with proper grooves where the joint design requires them.
- Make upper and lower shaft interface pieces replaceable wherever practical, so a future donor column or coupler change does not require cutting the adapter from the vehicle.
- Paint-mark final fasteners after torque.

Do not rely on casual welded steering-shaft joins. Any shaft welding or adapter machining must be treated as specialist work and inspected before road use.

Hold point: the system must steer manually with EPS off, without binding, sliding, or shaft play.

## 4) EPS ECU/Controller And Wiring

The J40 has no vehicle ECU, but the donor EPS still needs its matched EPS ECU/controller unless the column is proven integrated.

Required installation:

- Mount controller in a dry, supported, serviceable cabin location.
- If mounted on or near the General EPS Adapter, isolate the controller from motor heat/vibration and keep connector access visible after the lower dash is fitted.
- Keep it away from foot impact, water paths, heater leaks, and sharp brackets.
- Use donor pigtails only for connector identification and bench testing.
- Build final power, ground, ignition trigger, fuse protection, and loom with new automotive cable and terminals.
- Protect every pass-through with a grommet or bulkhead fitting.
- Label EPS fuse, trigger feed, ground, and controller connector.
- Photograph the wiring before wrapping or tying the loom closed.

Hold point: no permanent loom closure and no final adapter paint/trim closure until powered assist works, voltage drop/ground integrity are checked, and the controller can still be unplugged without removing the whole adapter.

## 5) Fabrication Change Register

The new General EPS Adapter route changes the old plan as follows:

| Area | Previous assumption | Current control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Donor bracket use | Adapt donor Vitz/Yaris brackets directly where practical | Donor brackets are references only; final fit is through the General EPS Adapter |
| Column support | Fabricate a one-off support cradle around the EPS body | Fabricate a removable adapter package with datum plate, saddle/clamp, doubler, spacers, and replaceable donor-side interface |
| Shaft adaptation | Build a controlled intermediate shaft path | Build the shaft path to the adapter datum, with replaceable upper/lower coupler interfaces and no casual welded joins |
| Firewall/cowl load | Add doubler/spreader where needed | Firewall doubler and load-spread path are part of the adapter release, not optional afterthoughts |
| Controller mount | Dry serviceable cabin location | Prefer serviceable adapter-adjacent carrier only if it does not add heat/vibration or connector-access problems |
| Fabrication stock | 3 mm bracket stock starting point | 3 mm steel remains valid; add 4 mm plate/angle, gussets, spacers, and sleeve stock where mock-up proves load or bolt-through needs |
| Validation | Manual fallback, powered assist, lock-to-lock checks | Same validation, plus adapter rigidity, fastener access, replacement coupler access, and post-first-drive adapter recheck |

## 6) Validation Before Road Use

Static checks:

- Front axle safely unloaded.
- Steering lock-to-lock with EPS off.
- Steering lock-to-lock with EPS powered.
- No shaft bind, joint notchiness, motor rocking, adapter flex, or wiring movement.
- Pedals, knees, dash, heater, and wiring clear through full movement.
- Firewall seal/grommet fitted.
- Adapter fasteners, bracket fasteners, pinch bolts, couplers, controller mount, ground, and fuse paint-marked.
- Upper and lower shaft interface pieces can be inspected without dismantling unrelated dash or pedal hardware.

Low-speed checks:

- Yard turn test.
- Steering return behavior.
- Braking while turning.
- Recheck adapter movement, bracket movement, shaft play, and wiring after the first movement.

Road release:

- Alignment complete.
- No steering bind or unpredictable assist.
- Manual fallback behavior understood.
- Recheck after short drive, then again after normal road validation.
- Log any adapter fastener retorque, witness-mark movement, new noise, or clearance rub before declaring final closeout.
