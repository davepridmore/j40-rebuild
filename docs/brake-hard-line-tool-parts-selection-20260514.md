## Reject Rules

- No single flare for brake lines.
- No ISO bubble flare unless a removed original line proves that exact port uses it.
- No bare copper, plumbing tube, compression fittings, or generic rubber hose.
- No final line fabricated until the shop/tool produces clean practice double/inverted flares on the selected tube.
- No old hydraulic port left open; cap immediately after removal.
