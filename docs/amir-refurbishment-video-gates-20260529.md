# Amir Refurbishment Video Gates - 2026-05-29

Purpose: define what Amir must collect on video before any payment or purchase for sample-controlled refurbishment jobs. Amir is the runner; the mechanic/user approves the work from the sample, measurements, and test evidence.

## Global Rule

No payment, deposit, exchange-unit purchase, or final collection unless the shop sends usable videos. Photos are useful, but videos are the release gate.

Each video set must show:

- The old sample part in the same frame as the shop/counter item or refurbished item.
- A slow 360 degree view, not just one face.
- Critical mounting, fitting, and interface points close enough to inspect.
- The test being performed on the actual part, not a verbal claim after the fact.
- Price, shop name/contact, and whether the part is repair of our old sample, exchange refurbished stock, or a newly made unit.

Reject any quote where the shop will not show the actual sample, the actual candidate/refurbished item, and the actual test.

## Engine Radiator Video Gate

Before payment or approval, Amir must send these videos:

| Video | Must show |
| --- | --- |
| Old sample identity | Full old radiator, top/bottom tanks, cap neck, overflow nipple, drain plug, upper/lower hose necks, side/top/bottom mounts, shroud/bracket holes, and the extra fabricated support leg that was added because the radiator was not installed correctly. |
| Measurement pass | Tape or caliper on overall height, width, thickness, upper neck OD, lower neck OD, cap neck position, key mounting hole spacing, lower pad landings, and factory-side/top mounting points. |
| Shop decision | Rebuilder explaining whether they will recore the old tanks/brackets or build a new copper/brass radiator from the sample, with the old unit visible. |
| Pressure / leak test | Gauge and radiator visible during test; water/air test must show no leaks or bubbling. Shop should state the test pressure and it must be appropriate for the fitted radiator cap. |
| Core / build proof | New core or finished radiator visible; show core thickness/row depth, soldered tank seams, necks, drain, overflow, and mounting tabs. |
| Mounting correction proof | Old extra leg shown as a warning only; video must show how the radiator will sit on proper lower pads/side/top mounts without relying on that added leg or pulling the front support out of alignment. If the aluminium radiator has already been bought and does not land correctly, video must show the proposed removable adapter/cradle, rubber isolation points, and vehicle-side attachment before fabrication/payment. If vehicle-side mounts are missing/damaged, that becomes a separate vehicle bracket repair. |
| Final acceptance video | Finished unit next to old sample or old-sample photos, with necks, mounts, cap, drain, corrected mount plan, and pressure test shown before final payment/collection. |

Reject if hose neck positions change, mounting tabs are missing, the shop wants to copy the extra fabricated leg as the main support, the proposed aluminium-radiator fix hard-mounts or stresses the tank/core, the drain/cap/overflow are omitted, the shop cannot pressure-test, the core is visibly weak or second-hand, or the repair relies only on paint/sealant over old leaks.

## Brake Booster / Servo Video Gate

Before payment, exchange purchase, or final refurbished-unit collection, Amir must send these videos:

| Video | Must show |
| --- | --- |
| Old booster identity | Full old booster/servo, shell diameter/depth, firewall studs, master-cylinder studs, center bore/seat, pedal pushrod, clevis, boot, vacuum check valve/grommet, and any markings. |
| Sample match | Old booster beside the candidate/refurbished unit or old booster before/after rebuild; compare firewall studs, master studs, pushrod/clevis, shell diameter/depth, and check-valve position. |
| Interface close-up | Master-cylinder mounting face, pushrod tip/depth, clevis thread and pin hole, firewall bracket/studs, check-valve/grommet, and hose barb direction. |
| Vacuum hold test | Hand vacuum pump or bench tester connected to the actual booster, gauge visible, no obvious leakdown for at least 60 seconds, and no hissing. |
| Assist movement test | Pushrod/diaphragm action while vacuum is applied, with smooth return and no sticking. |
| Contamination check | Master-cylinder side opened/visible enough to show no brake-fluid pooling inside the booster and no rotten seal debris. |
| Final acceptance video | Refurbished/matched unit with all studs, clevis/pushrod, check valve/grommet, boots, and mounting faces shown before payment/collection. |

Reject if it is a raw untested used booster, single-diaphragm/drum-brake unit, welded or hammered shell, wrong stud pattern, wrong pushrod/clevis, missing check valve/grommet, visible brake-fluid contamination, hissing, or any vacuum leakdown the shop cannot explain and fix.

## Payment Rule

Amir can collect prices and shop cards freely. Payment is only approved after the video set is reviewed and the chosen route is explicitly accepted:

- radiator: recore our sample, or build a new copper/brass radiator by sample;
- brake booster: rebuild our old booster, or supply a professionally refurbished direct-match exchange unit.
