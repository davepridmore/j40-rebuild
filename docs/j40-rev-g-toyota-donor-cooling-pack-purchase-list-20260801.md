# J40 Rev G — simple donor-parts purchase list

**Issue:** 1 August 2026
**Vehicle:** 1978 Toyota J40, Toyota 2H, turbo cooling-design ceiling 150 bhp / 112 kW, R134a A/C
**Cooling target:** 50°C air at the grille, subject to the installed tests in the controlled fabricator specification
**Use with:** `J40-integrated-cooling-pack-fabricator-specification-rev-c.md`

## Take these measurements to the parts market first

Bring one rigid 1:1 template that copies **both original top holes together**, marked LEFT / RIGHT / FRONT. Also bring the signed measurements below. Do not buy the final radiator from a catalogue size.

| ID | Required vehicle/sample measurement |
|---|---|
| B0 | Both existing top-hole diameters/condition/edge distance/tab thickness and centre-to-centre |
| H0-L / H0-R | Each top-hole plane to its lower-saddle seat |
| W0 | Smallest clear width between post inner faces at top/middle/bottom, including weld beads |
| P0 | Top-return/post plane to the proposed radiator side rail |
| R0 | **Complete radiator W × H × D**, including tanks, seams, filler, drain, ears and lower locators |

Existing bracket reference only: **48 mm main face / 410 mm upright / 58 mm inward top return**. Confirm these on the vehicle. The 410 mm dimension is **not radiator height**.

## C. Fabricate locally only after measurement

- One removable radiator side rail per side.
- Two short **4 mm direct ears**, one immediately below each original top-return hole.
- Two lower rubber-lined saddles directly under those same radiator side rails.
- If the sample radiator ears cannot reach, two small independent rubber-isolated adapter plates—one each side. **No cross-car radiator carrier.**
- One centred sealed twin-pusher shroud and one full-face rear mechanical-fan shroud if the Toyota shroud cannot fit.
- Independent mounts for charge cooler and condenser; simple edge seals and removable stone screen.
- **Two separate small structural electrical brackets**, staggered vertically/depth-wise, plus two individual rain hoods. No common electrical plate and no load on radiator, side rails, saddles or original top returns.
- Measured hose/pipe adapters, P-clips and corrosion protection.

## Do not buy or fabricate yet

- No final radiator/core until B0/H0-L/H0-R/W0/P0/R0 and the D08 rigid fixture/full-size dummy are signed.
- No Prado radiator or complete Prado/GX donor shroud.
- No generic fan without its physical motor/blade/plug/depth sample.
- No third electric fan: the system has exactly **two front electric pushers plus one rear engine mechanical puller**.
- No used/open receiver-drier.
- No shared 525 mm electrical tray, top crossbar, slotted chassis hole or forced bolt alignment.

## Pakistan availability check

These checks support a practical local sourcing route; stock and condition still change, so the physical sample controls.

- **Prado donor parts — strong local inference:** complete Prado vehicles and used Toyota parts are commonly advertised in Pakistan; buying two matching fan sets from vehicle breakers/Bilal Ganj is realistic. [Example Prado vehicle listing](https://www.pakwheels.com/used-cars/toyota-prado-2009-for-sale-in-karachi-11655890) and [Toyota used relay/fuse-box listing](https://www.pakwheels.com/accessories-spare-parts/2pcs-toyota-fuse-box-relay-genuine-used-kabli-11010789).
- **Prado radiator listings exist, but do not buy one for this design:** they confirm Toyota cooling-part supply, not J40 bracket fit. [Pakistan Koyorad Prado listing](https://www.pakwheels.com/accessories-spare-parts/koyorad-radiator-for-toyota-prado-2003-2015-11755872).
- **HJ47/2H parts — part-reference verified, local stock not confirmed online:** use the numbers to identify samples, but expect used/imported supply or local recore. [HJ47 radiator catalogue](https://www.megazip.net/zapchasti-dlya-avtomobilej/toyota/land-cruiser-38286/hj47-56585/hj47rv-kcq-936138/radiator-water-outlet-18022835) and [Toyota shroud reference](https://yoshiparts.com/parts/toyota-1671147040).
- **Universal A/C hardware — locally listed:** a 14 × 23 condenser is advertised locally and new receiver-driers are listed by Pakistani suppliers; both must be port- and envelope-matched. [Local condenser listing](https://www.olx.com.pk/item/universal-condenser-for-car-air-conditioner-iid-1067408639) and [new receiver-driers](https://hafizbrothers.pk/product-category/receiver-drier/).
- **Exact charge-cooler size — no current Pakistan listing confirmed:** buy from a turbo/fabrication supplier only after measuring the actual unit; do not accept “approximately the same” without recording its complete envelope.
