## 1. Simple instruction for the radiator shop

Reuse and refurbish the actual project parts:

1. **R0:** original copper/brass engine-coolant radiator.
2. **C0:** original broad A/C condenser with its pipes, receiver/drier and brackets.
3. **FL:** original large electric fan and full shroud; intended as the rear puller only if its label, condition, current, rotation and airflow tests pass.
4. **FS:** original small electric fan; reuse at the front only if its complete measured frame fits the symmetric twin-fan limit below.
5. **G0:** original removable stone guard.
6. **E0:** existing aligned relay/MIDI-fuse carrier; add capacity inside the same silhouette and keep all live studs under a lid.

Add only:

- **K0:** one shallow, low-centred turbo charge-air cooler (intercooler/CAC); and
- either one true matching small Toyota/Denso fan, or two matching Toyota/Denso fans if no true mate is available or the measured pair misses the released envelope.

“Add only” above describes the functional heat exchangers and fans; it does not omit the new hoses, charge pipes, mounting rubbers, A/C service parts, covered protection, wiring, fasteners, seals and commissioning materials required in Section 9.

The required air path is:

**grille → stone guard → two equal centred front electric pushers → low-centred K0 → full-face C0 → full-face R0 → sealed FL electric puller/shroud → engine**

There are three electric fans in the finished pack. There is one engine-water radiator (R0), one A/C condenser (C0) and one turbo intercooler (K0). Do not add a second water radiator or a second condenser.

## 3. Centred front-fan release

The photos support a useful first full-size dummy, but not a purchase or cutting size:

- photographed FS complete frame/tabs: about **F = 280 mm**;
- two equal frames plus preferred centre gap **g = 10 mm**: about **570 mm** complete pair width; and
- photographed R0 left-hook datum to right rail: about **635 mm**, provisionally giving about **32.5 mm** at each side when the pair is centred.

This makes the retained FS plus one true matching fan a credible first choice. The circular guard is only about 240–255 mm, however, and the swept blade diameter is smaller again. The fan pair must therefore be checked against both the complete vehicle/radiator envelope and the usable ducted core face; the approximate 540 mm C0 body dimension alone is not the fan-carrier width.

For the measured vehicle fixture, define:

- **Wc:** usable centred carrier width inside both the measured vehicle opening W0 and the complete R0 silhouette;
- **F:** complete width of one fan module, including frame, tabs, motor, plug and required cable bend;
- **Db:** actual swept blade diameter, excluding the guard/frame;
- **Wa:** measured active or properly sealed/ducted heat-exchanger face width;
- **g:** clear gap between complete modules, preferably 10 mm; and
- **M = (Wc − 2F − g) / 2:** equal left/right carrier margin.

Release the pair only when:

- **2F + g ≤ Wc**, M is positive and left/right margins match within 2 mm;
- the two complete modules, labels, guards, motors, plugs and mounting tabs are physically identical;
- both fan centres are level within 2 mm and their midpoint is on the vehicle centreline within 2 mm;
- **2Db + g ≤ Wa**, unless a tested sealed plenum demonstrates even flow over the required face;
- both push air from grille toward engine at their installed polarity;
- no frame, plug, cable bend or electrical item projects beyond the released W0/R0 front-view envelope; and
- the complete three-fan cooling pack delivers at least **3,000 m³/h installed airflow at 13.5 V at the fan terminals**, with **3,300 m³/h preferred**, through the finished guard/core stack.

Do not add free-air catalogue figures together. Measure installed system airflow or pressure/velocity over a defined face.

Prefer one exact tested FS mate because that reuses the existing part and gives the symmetric appearance requested. If no exact mate exists, either fan fails, or the complete measured pair misses Wc/Wa, buy two matching Toyota/Denso modules and retain FS as a tested spare. Do not cut a safety guard, mix different fans, or rely on a donor vehicle name as proof of fit.

## 4. Heat-exchanger layout and starting envelope

### R0 engine radiator

- Retain the original tanks, filler/cap, necks, rails, ears and lower locators if sound.
- Clean, inspect, pressure-test and flow-test it before deciding whether to rod, repair or recore.
- It remains the only engine-coolant radiator.
- Use the photo-backed 610 mm body and 635 mm cap heights for the first physical fixture.
- Final width, depth, neck map, ear coordinates and locator coordinates remain HOLD.

### C0 A/C condenser

- Retain the full approximately 540 × 465 mm condenser body, pipes and brackets if they pass flush, fin and pressure checks. Keep the photographed receiver/drier/manifold as the mock-up geometry sample.
- Because the A/C circuit is expected to be opened for this work, fit a **new** compatible receiver/drier/desiccant element and new seals at final assembly; never install a used donor drier. If the drier is integral, the A/C specialist must specify the correct service element/procedure.
- Mount it directly in front of R0 on independent removable brackets.
- Do not crop it, turn it into a half-width unit, or hang K0 from it.

### K0 turbo CAC

Use a cardboard starting box of approximately:

- **450–500 mm core width**;
- **150–180 mm core height**; and
- **no more than about 65 mm core depth**.

This is not a purchase dimension. Tanks, ports, beads, brackets and charge-pipe bends make the complete unit larger. Buy K0 only after the opaque 1:1 vehicle fixture proves its complete envelope low and centred, within W0, without blocking service access or extending at either side.

## 5. Mounting and load path

R0 must be visibly attached at the intended top holes and fully supported below:

**R0 lower locators → two Toyota-pattern rubber saddles → full-width lower crossmember → gusseted/removable connections → both structural side uprights**

- Both lower locators sit fully in rubber saddles. No tank, soldered seam or fin carries weight.
- Start the fixture with 40 × 40 × 3 mm mild-steel SHS only if clear span is no more than 700 mm and filled R0 mass is no more than 35 kg; otherwise recalculate before fabrication.
- Both existing inward-facing top holes must be used through short removable radiator ears, sleeved EPDM bushes, washers and locking nuts.
- The two top stays locate and restrain R0; they do not carry its filled weight.
- Make one rigid two-hole B0 template. Drill removable ears only after the actual R0 sits naturally on both saddles.
- Do not slot, ream, drill new chassis holes, side-bolt as a substitute, or pull the radiator into alignment.
- C0, K0, both front fans, FL/shroud and E0 each have independent removable supports.

Before paint, weigh R0 filled and capped. Proof-load the lower cradle at the two saddle centres to twice that mass for 10 minutes. PASS requires no permanent set, cracked weld, loosened fastener, saddle movement or upright distortion.

## 6. Three electric fans and covered protection

Treat FL as a retained electric fan because the new photo shows its motor and wiring. Do not call it mechanical.

- **F1:** left front pusher, independent relay/fuse/earth.
- **F2:** right front pusher, independent relay/fuse/earth.
- **FL:** large rear puller candidate, independent relay/fuse/earth.

For every fan, record label/part number, condition, start current, stabilized current, polarity, rotation, installed direction and voltage at the motor under load. FL remains behind R0 only if it pulls toward the engine in its designed direction and the sealed shroud clears the radiator, hoses, engine and service tools. Do not reverse a fan casually; blade and shroud design may be directional.

Use three separately protected branches sized from measured current and voltage drop. Retain E0’s current aligned inboard location if possible. If a third branch will not fit, extend the carrier upward or inward inside the released front-view silhouette; never add a side fuse annex. Every MIDI fuse, bus and live stud must be under a splash-resistant removable cover with down/rear cable exits and a proven lid/service sweep.

Suggested logic:

- A/C demand/high-side pressure may request F1/F2.
- Coolant temperature may stage F1/F2, then FL.
- High IAT/boost/high load may request F1/F2 and FL.
- High coolant temperature, high A/C pressure, sensor/control fault or manual emergency override commands all three.

Final logic must fail safe without hiding a cooling fault.

The final electrical purchase includes three correctly rated sealed relay/base positions, three covered MIDI/maxi branch-fuse positions, calculated main-feed protection, new automotive cable/earths/terminals/seals and a documented control strategy. Retain any sound E0 parts only after their ratings and condition are proven; buy only the missing positions needed to total three independent branches.

## 8. 50 °C and turbo performance release

The design target remains operation at 50 °C ambient without cooling-limited use inside the agreed 150 bhp turbo envelope. It is not achieved by dimensions alone.

Acceptance requires:

- R0 heat rejection at least **115 kW continuous** and **130 kW for 10 minutes** at the defined test duty;
- post-CAC intake temperature no higher than **80 °C** at the agreed boost/load point;
- complete charge-route pressure drop no higher than **10 kPa** at that point;
- no progressive coolant, IAT or A/C high-side-pressure rise at hot idle with A/C operating;
- stable system voltage, fan current, cable/fuse/relay temperature and installed airflow;
- no contact, leakage, vibration, blocked service access or boost/load derate caused by cooling inside the accepted envelope.

Instrument ambient, coolant inlet/outlet, pre/post-CAC IAT, boost, EGT, A/C high-side pressure, fan commands, system voltage and each fan current. Use a controlled 50 °C cell or a documented equivalent heat-soak/load simulation followed by the hottest available loaded road test.

If the low triple-overlap region causes progressive coolant temperature, A/C pressure or IAT, stop. First correct core condition, fan direction, sealing and ducting. If it still fails, relocate K0 to independently supplied air or engineer a water-to-air system under a new revision; do not shrink C0 or increase boost to hide the result.

## 10. Shop sequence

1. Put the actual R0, C0, FL/shroud, FS, G0, E0 and vehicle together.
2. Mark each part ID and record every HOLD dimension above.
3. Test R0, C0, FL and FS; stop or repair if a retained part fails.
4. Make the B0 and S0 rigid templates and the lower-load fixture.
5. Make two full-size fan dummies from the measured FS envelope (about 280 mm each as photographed), with the actual plug and cable-bend space, plus one complete K0 dummy.
6. Build the opaque full-size stack and prove width, depth, top holes, lower load path, service sweeps and removal paths.
7. Choose one FS mate only if the complete pair passes; otherwise choose two equal narrow donor fans.
8. Buy K0 and fabricate independent removable brackets only after the fixture passes.
9. Complete pressure/flow/electrical/installed-airflow tests, then the instrumented 50 °C acceptance test.
10. Record photos and results before paint and final sign-off.
