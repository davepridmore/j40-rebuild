## Buy Specification

Ask the supplier/shop for:

- Preferred: evaporator core/cooling coil already in a sealed compact case or plenum with outlet spigots or a flat face for a custom outlet plenum.
- Bare cooling coil only if the shop/fabricator has a clear sealed plenum, drain pan, and blower-adapter plan.
- R134a-compatible evaporator/TXV/fittings, or clear fitting sizes for new barrier hoses.
- Drain nipple with enough fall to route condensate outside the cabin.
- Case dimensions and photos from all sides before travel or deposit.
- Coil face width/height, core depth, outlet count and outlet OD, or a flat face that can accept a sealed plenum and 2 to 4 duct takeoffs.
- Vent plan compatibility: enough outlet area for four integrated dashboard outlets in the `1-2-1` layout, plus demist if required.
- External blower compatibility: adapter face, airflow across the full coil face, service access, and measured blower current once the project blowers are selected.
- Return-air path that can be fitted with a serviceable grille/filter.
- Bench airflow test with the selected external blowers plus nitrogen pressure/vacuum leak test before payment.
- A body envelope that can be cardboard-mocked in the J40 before buying; large OEM dash cases are rejected unless the mock-up proves fit.

Reject:

- Bare evaporator coils with no drain pan and no feasible sealed plenum path.
- 24V bus/truck units.
- Visible-only hang-on units that cannot be ducted or hidden cleanly.
- Large full-OEM dash cases with molded outlets and protruding blower scrolls unless measured mock-up proves fit.
- Cracked cases, missing drain, oil staining, corroded core, blocked fittings, or no way to seal the external blowers to the coil face.
- Any final A/C hose crimping before the evaporator, condenser, compressor, receiver-drier, and firewall bulkhead positions are fixed.
