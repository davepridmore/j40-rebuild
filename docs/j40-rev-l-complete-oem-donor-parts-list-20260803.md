# J40 Rev L — complete OEM/donor-first cooling-pack parts list

**Issue:** 3 August 2026<br>
**Use with:** `J40-photo-measured-retained-cooling-pack-specification-rev-l.md` and drawings D19/D20<br>
**Rule:** take the cleaned real parts, their labels/plugs and the completed measurement sheet to the Toyota breaker and radiator shop. A donor vehicle name is only a place to search; it is never proof that a part fits.

> **Karigar ke liye:** R0 radiator, C0 condenser, FL bara fan/shroud, FS chhota fan, stone guard aur existing covered fuse/relay position workshop mein saath lao. Fan, rubber saddle, bush, hose ya plug sirf gaari ke naam par na khareedo—asal sample aur poora W × H × D match karo. Drier, O-rings, hoses, couplers, clamps, cable, terminals, relays, fuses aur coolant **naye** hon. Pehlay cardboard/steel dummy fit karo, phir final maal khareedo.

## The short shopping answer

- **Reuse after test:** the one original R0 engine radiator, one full-face C0 A/C condenser, one large FL rear electric fan/shroud, one small FS front electric fan, stone guard and the existing inboard covered relay/MIDI carrier.
- **Find from Toyota/Denso donor stock:** preferably one exact mate to the retained FS, giving two installed front fans in total; otherwise buy two identical complete front fan modules and keep FS as the spare. Also sample-match Toyota-pattern radiator rubbers, fan plugs/grommets and an overflow bottle only if the original is missing or failed.
- **Buy new:** K0 intercooler if no pressure-tested donor core meets the measured box; charge pipes/couplers/clamps; cooling hoses/cap/thermostat/coolant; A/C receiver/drier and opened-joint seals; all high-current electrical consumables; mounting rubber, fasteners, seals and coating materials.
- **Fabricate locally:** the full-width lower R0 support, two saddle seats, two short upper locating stays, centred twin-fan carrier, independent C0 and K0 supports/ducts, rear-shroud attachment and any inward/upward covered electrical-carrier extension.
- **Buy only if testing requires it:** replacement R0/C0/FL, two replacement front fans, overflow bottle, or a higher-output Toyota/Denso alternator.

The finished pack has exactly **one engine radiator R0, one A/C condenser C0, one turbo intercooler K0 and three electric fans**. Do not buy a second water radiator or a second condenser.

## C. Local fabrication scope

Fabricate only after the opaque 1:1 fixture passes:

1. full-width R0 lower crossmember and removable/gusseted connections to both structural side uprights;
2. two lower saddle seats matching R1 and the S0 locator template;
3. two short removable upper ears/stays using both existing top holes through R2 sleeves/bushes;
4. one centred carrier for the two equal complete front fan modules, their plugs and seals;
5. independent removable C0 brackets with A3 isolators;
6. low-centred removable K0 cradle, M1 isolators, duct and supported K1 charge-pipe route;
7. sealed removable FL rear-shroud attachment; and
8. covered E0 three-branch carrier extension upward or inward only, inside the existing silhouette.

No core, tank, pipe, fan frame or electrical box may carry the weight of another heat exchanger or R0.

## D. Workshop services and commissioning materials

These are required even though they may be supplied as workshop services rather than permanent vehicle parts:

- R0 clean, pressure test, flow test and any earned repair/recore;
- C0 clean, flush and dry-nitrogen/approved-gas pressure/leak test;
- K0 and complete charge route pressure/leak test;
- fan bench current/polarity/direction test, then installed current/voltage-drop/airflow test at 13.5 V at the fan terminals;
- cooling-system flush, vacuum/fill or controlled bleed, pressure test and cap test;
- A/C evacuation, vacuum hold/leak test and recharge by specified mass;
- temporary or permanent calibrated measurement of ambient, coolant inlet/outlet, pre/post-CAC IAT, boost, EGT, A/C high-side pressure, system voltage and each fan current; and
- 50 °C-equivalent hot-idle and loaded acceptance with all three fans, A/C and turbo duty represented.

## E. Do not buy yet

- K0 or charge pipe before W0, P0-upper, P0-lower, port directions and complete bends are proven;
- a fan described only as “Prado”, “Land Cruiser”, “Hilux” or “HiAce” without physical label/frame/plug/rotation matching;
- one mismatched fan merely because its blade diameter looks similar;
- final coolant or charge hoses before ports, engine movement and service sweeps are fixed;
- any fuse, relay, controller, alternator or cable size before start/run current, route length, hot-idle load and voltage-drop calculations;
- a used receiver/drier, old silicone couplers, unknown radiator cap/thermostat, used high-current cable or anonymous fuse holder;
- a second water radiator, replacement half-width condenser, side electrical annex or unapproved fourth fan; or
- final paint before the lower-support proof load, dry-fit and service/removal checks pass.

## F. Before payment, write this on the invoice or inspection sheet

- manufacturer and complete part number/label photograph;
- donor model/year/engine only as traceability, not as fit proof;
- complete W × H × D including tabs, tanks, ports, motor, plug and cable bend;
- fan direction at installed polarity and measured start/run current;
- hose/pipe internal and outside diameters, bend map and bead/clamp sizes;
- pressure-test rating/result for K0 and any supplied C0/R0 work;
- electrical continuous/current-interrupt ratings and enclosure/cover details;
- return/exchange agreement if the physical fixture fails; and
- which actual sample, B0/S0 template or released drawing was matched.

Final fit remains **HOLD** until the real assembly sits on both lower saddles, uses both intended top holes and fits the measured vehicle width/depth with grille, bumper, latch, engine movement, pipes, plugs, lids and service tools represented. The 50 °C/no-cooling-derate claim remains **HOLD** until the installed three-fan pack passes airflow, electrical, A/C, coolant and turbo-temperature testing.
