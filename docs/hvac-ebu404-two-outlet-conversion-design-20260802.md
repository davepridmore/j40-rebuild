# EBU-404 Two-Outlet Concealed HVAC Conversion — Rev A

- Date: 2026-08-02
- Donor: Gcfix `EBU-404-100 / BEU-404-100`, ordered as 12 V cool-and-heat
- Objective: retain the donor coil, blower, drain, thermostat/freeze control and heater core, but replace the four-louvre discharge face with a sealed two-duct plenum feeding two supported branches to the high outer/end J40 fascia outlets
- Status: concept released for measurement and cardboard mock-up only; no production cutting until the received unit is opened and the starred dimensions below are recorded

## Design decision

Use the ordered unit as the donor. Do **not** cap two of its four outlets. Remove the four louvres and, if the casing permits, remove or cut back the molded outlet deck to expose the complete blower discharge opening. Fit a shallow, removable transition plenum with two `70 mm OD` beaded hose spigots.

The listing confirms:

| Donor feature | Published value |
| --- | ---: |
| Overall width | 400 mm |
| Overall depth | 320 mm |
| Overall height | 140 mm |
| Existing outlet neck | 50 mm |
| Existing outlets | 4 |
| Weight | 5 kg |
| Capacity | about 13,092 BTU/h |
| Coil | 32-pass, four-way aluminium/copper |
| Refrigerant fittings | #6 male O-ring liquid; #10 male O-ring suction |
| Refrigerant | R134a or R12 stated; build the J40 system for R134a |

Four `50 mm` outlets provide `7,854 mm²` total nominal flow area. Two `70 mm` outlets provide `7,697 mm²`, or about `98%` of the original area. Two `63 mm` outlets provide only `79%`; two `50 mm` outlets halve the area and are rejected.

### Dashboard-outlet interface

The two `70 mm OD` plenum spigots are the donor-side duct standard, not the dashboard-louver mounting size. The active Rev I V35 target replaces only the ashtray/centre area with a removable, zero-drop cassette; the OEM glovebox, cluster, RHD column scallop, full-width lower edge and formed contours remain unchanged. Any future cassette-only extension requires physical measurement and owner approval. Its dashboard target family is:

| Dashboard interface | Target/reference | Rule |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Visible outlet face | Ø87 mm | Front-view appearance and clearance only |
| Rear mounting boss/barrel | Ø75 mm target maximum | Not a hose neck and not a released panel aperture |
| Rear louver-body projection | ≤ approximately 22 mm | Finished rear fascia plane to louver body only |
| Hose spigot | Unknown until M7 | Measure OD, ID, projection, bead and usable engagement |

Do not stretch a 70 mm hose over the Ø75 mounting boss or assume the outlet accepts a 70 mm hose. After receipt, provide a smooth sealed adapter from each 70 mm plenum branch to the measured dashboard-outlet spigot. Keep the adapter transition gradual, preserve the proven total flow area, and include the vent retainer, adapter, clamp and first bend in the full-depth mock-up and airflow/freeze test.

## Proposed geometry

### Fixed design dimensions

| Feature | Rev A dimension |
| --- | ---: |
| New hose neck outside diameter | 70 mm |
| Neck straight length | 40 mm minimum |
| Retaining bead | 2 mm high, 6 mm behind end |
| Outlet centre positions on 400 mm datum | X = 115 mm and 285 mm |
| Outlet centre spacing | 170 mm |
| Outlet centre height | centre on the measured discharge window |
| Maximum new plenum width / height | 400 / 140 mm |
| Target transition depth, cut plane to port plate | 65–75 mm |
| Transition wall angle | 30 degrees maximum where practicable |
| External insulation | 6 mm closed-cell elastomeric foam |
| Seal | 3 mm closed-cell foam gasket, mechanically fastened |

The `65–75 mm` plenum depth is a starting envelope, not a promise of a smaller complete unit. The donor's rear blower bulge determines most of the published `320 mm` depth. The conversion should remove the visible louvres and prevent extra console bulk; it may save only the depth of the existing outlet deck until the casing is physically measured.

### Measure before cutting

Record these dimensions directly on the received unit:

- `A*`: complete discharge-window width after the louvres/outlet deck are removed.
- `B*`: complete discharge-window height.
- `C*`: distance from the proposed cut plane to the nearest coil, heater core, blower wheel, wiring or thermostat capillary.
- `D*`: case-wall thickness and the screw/boss locations that can accept a removable flange.
- `E*`: depth from the proposed cut plane to the rearmost blower cover.
- `F*`: drain nipple position, OD and required installed fall.
- `G*`: refrigerant, heater and control exit positions and their service envelopes.
- `H*`: actual fascia-outlet-side hose direction, measured outlet-spigot OD/ID and the shortest bend radius available for each 70 mm branch and any required adapter.

Release rule: the plenum inlet must cover the whole `A* × B*` discharge opening. Leave at least `15 mm` gasket land around it and at least `10 mm` between any fastener and an internal core or tube. If those lands do not fit inside the existing 400 × 140 mm face, use an external wrap-around clamping flange rather than narrowing the opening.

## Construction

Preferred locally fabricable version:

- Body: `1.0–1.2 mm` aluminium sheet, folded and TIG welded or sealed-riveted outside the wet airflow path.
- Port plate: `1.2–1.5 mm` aluminium.
- Two rolled aluminium spigots, `70 mm OD × 40 mm`, with a retaining bead.
- Split the enclosure into a fixed transition and a removable port plate if that is needed to reach the evaporator for cleaning.
- Smooth every internal edge; no exposed rivet mandrels, sealant strings or sharp steps in the airstream.
- Apply `6 mm` closed-cell elastomeric insulation to every external cold surface. Do not block the condensate tray or drain.
- Join to the donor with M4 screws, large backing washers or captive strip, and a continuous `3 mm` closed-cell gasket. Do not rely on silicone as the structural fastener.

ABS alternative: vacuum-form or heat-form `2.5–3 mm` ABS over a buck, plastic-weld the spigots, and use the same mechanical gasketed flange. Aluminium is the easier one-off prototype for a Lahore/Rawalpindi sheet-metal shop.

## Internal arrangement to retain

The finished donor module must still contain and perform these functions:

1. Return-air intake and washable filter access.
2. Blower and its three speeds or controller.
3. Evaporator coil and TXV/refrigerant metering hardware.
4. Thermostat/freeze-sensing capillary or probe in its original coil position.
5. Condensate tray and continuously falling drain.
6. Heater core, water pipes and a positive hot-water shutoff valve if heat is retained.
7. A sealed discharge path from the whole coil/blower opening to both 70 mm ducts.

Relocate the blower and temperature controls to the custom console, extending wiring only with serviceable automotive connectors. Do not shorten, kink or relocate a mechanical thermostat capillary unless its construction explicitly allows it.

## Fabrication and installation plan

### Gate 1 — Baseline the intact donor

- Photograph all six faces, label and wiring, fittings, drain and controls with a ruler.
- Run the blower from a fused 12 V supply at each speed. Record current and outlet air velocity at all four open louvres.
- If possible, have an A/C shop nitrogen-pressure-test the coil before the casing is altered.
- Pour water into the tray and prove the drain clears it without pooling.

### Gate 2 — Non-destructive strip and survey

- Remove only screws/clips first. Extract the four louvres and separate the case halves without cutting.
- Mark the evaporator, heater core, blower wheel, tubes, wiring and thermostat probe on the outside of the shell.
- Record `A*–H*`. Decide whether the molded outlet deck unbolts or must be cut.
- If cutting is required, remove or fully shield the cores, tubes and wiring. Do not cut blind with the refrigeration hardware behind the blade.

### Gate 3 — Cardboard plenum and vehicle mock-up

- Make a full-size cardboard transition with two 70 mm paper tubes at X = 115/285 mm.
- Mock the module, both hoses, console outlets, glovebox opening, steering column, knees and all lever sweeps in the J40.
- Keep each duct as short as practical, support it every 250–300 mm, and avoid a bend radius smaller than about `1.5 × hose ID` (105 mm centreline radius).
- Retain the original heater/demist route separately; these two ducts are the occupant outlets, not the only windscreen-clearing system.

### Gate 4 — Fabricate and seal

- Transfer the verified cardboard pattern to aluminium.
- Tack, trial-fit and check service access before final welding/riveting.
- Fit the continuous gasket and mechanically fasten the plenum. Add external insulation after leak and fit checks.

### Gate 5 — Bench acceptance

- With both 70 mm ducts fitted at their vehicle lengths, total measured airflow must be at least `85%` of the stock four-louvre baseline at the same voltage and blower speed.
- Left/right duct velocity must be within `15%` with both console vents fully open.
- Blower current must stay within `10%` of the stock baseline and the wheel must not surge, rub or whistle.
- Run cooling for at least 30 minutes: no progressive coil icing, no air leaks, no sweating on insulated outer surfaces and no water outside the drain.
- Repeat the drain test with the module pitched `5 degrees` fore/aft and left/right around its installed attitude.

### Gate 6 — Final installation

- Use rubber-isolated brackets attached to real case structure, not the thin plenum skin.
- Preserve removal access for the filter, blower motor, TXV/fittings, heater pipes, thermostat and drain.
- Crimp refrigerant hoses only after the installed module, condenser, drier, compressor and firewall pass-throughs are fixed.
- Complete nitrogen leak test, deep vacuum, measured R134a charge, freeze cutout, condensate, electrical-load, outlet balance and demist tests before closing the console.

## Stop conditions

Stop and redesign rather than fabricate if the complete discharge opening cannot feed both ports without a sharp internal obstruction; if cutting would approach a core/tube within 10 mm; if the drain cannot retain continuous fall; if 70 mm hoses cannot be routed without crushing below 90% of their diameter; or if the converted airflow is below the Gate 5 threshold.
