# J40 Chassis Welder Instruction — J80 Power Steering and 2H-117299 Turbo Provision

> **Superseded 2026-08-02 — do not issue this J80 conversion sheet to the welder.** The fitted gear is identified with high confidence as a Toyoda Koki late-40-Series factory-type hydraulic unit, and the owner-confirmed replacement plan is the component-first RHD J60/HJ60 upgrade. Do not procure a J80 box or drill/weld J80 conversion mounts.

**Do not drill or weld steering conversion mounts.** Clean, inspect and measure the fitted gear, pedestal, frame attachment, input shaft/coupler, pitman arm and linkage. Use that installed steering envelope, together with the physically verified 2H pump drive and selected turbo/manifold hardware, for the turbo trial fit.

1. Clean and photograph the bare steering-box frame area; report cracks, elongated holes, corrosion thinning and previous repairs.
2. Do not reuse the J60 plate/template or hole pattern by assumption. Clamp a new full-size template made from the actual J80 box.
3. Mock the box, matched pitman arm, drag-link line, supported collapsible shaft, pump/brackets and hose ports before marking the chassis.
4. Keep the battery tray adjustable. The approximately `300 x 205 mm` DLS120 footprint fits its `340 x 265 mm` tray, but a 90-degree bay orientation is not confirmed.
5. Prove box mount-bolt/tool access, shaft clearance, pitman lock-to-lock sweep, drag-link movement, hose bend/service space and battery lift-out.
6. Only then mock the turbo, downpipe, heat shield and engine-roll envelope.
7. Return measured templates, photographs and a marked-up gate sheet for engineering release before drilling, sleeves, gussets or final welding.
