# J40 retained-original cooling pack — Rev J

**Issue:** 2 August 2026<br>
**Vehicle/duty:** Toyota J40, Toyota 2H turbo plan, 150 bhp maximum, 50 °C ambient acceptance<br>
**Shop rule:** build from the physical vehicle and retained parts. Dimensions marked `MEASURE` are completed on the 1:1 fixture before steel or cores are ordered. AI images are explanatory only.

## 1. The design in one minute

Retain and refurbish the actual wide copper/brass engine radiator **R0**, its large rear 2H mechanical fan/shroud, removable stone guard, existing front electric fan, existing A/C condenser if serviceable, and covered relay/MIDI-fuse arrangement. Install two matching front pushers, add only one charge-air cooler (CAC), and make only the smallest removable carriers/seals needed to assemble the pack.

There are exactly **three installed fans**:

1. two **matching, equal-size front electric pushers**, horizontally symmetric and centred as one group on vehicle centreline `C0`; and
2. the retained large 2H engine-driven mechanical puller behind R0.

Use the existing front electric fan as the physical sample and source one matching Toyota/Denso-family complete module if it passes condition, rotation, current and airflow checks. If a genuine matching twin cannot be found, or the resulting pair fails fit/electrical/installed-airflow/50 °C acceptance, install a physically matched Toyota/Denso pair and retain the old fan as a service spare. **Unequal front fans are not permitted.**

Air path, grille to engine:

**removable stone guard → two matching front pushers → one shallow split auxiliary plane (CAC beside A/C condenser) → full-width R0 engine radiator → sealed rear shroud → retained large mechanical puller → engine**.

The CAC and condenser are **side-by-side, not one behind the other**. Each sits in the swept/ducted zone of its respective front fan. R0 spans the full opening behind both zones.

> *Karigar ke liye: purana bara radiator hi engine ka radiator rahe ga. Aagay do aik-jaisay electric pankhay bilkul beech mein lagain. Un ke peechay turbo cooler aur A/C condenser aik hi satah par side-by-side hon; aik doosray ke peechay nahin. Sab se peechay purana bara mechanical fan rahe ga.*

## 2. Why there are three heat exchangers

They do separate jobs and cannot replace one another:

| Heat exchanger | Fluid/circuit | Rev J treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R0 engine radiator | Engine coolant | Retain/refurbish; full width behind the auxiliary plane. Recore only when inspection or thermal testing earns it. |
| A/C condenser | R134a refrigerant | Retain if identification, pressure, flush, fin and fit checks pass; otherwise use a physically checked replacement. |
| CAC/intercooler | Compressed turbo charge air | Add one compact CAC. Size and ports are released only after the vehicle, pipe route and side-by-side envelope are measured. |

The side-by-side auxiliary arrangement reduces stack depth and avoids forcing all airflow through both auxiliary cores in series. It does not remove the need for either circuit.

## 4. Matched front-fan dimensions and centring

Do not release fixed fan coordinates from a catalogue or AI image. Put the two complete accepted modules on the 1:1 vehicle fixture.

- `Wc` = usable carrier/opening width between released obstructions;
- `F` = outside width of each matching complete fan module, including its frame/guard but excluding only a separately measured service bend;
- `g` = released clear gap between the two complete modules; use **10–15 mm preferred**, or more only when a measured divider/fastener requires it;
- `M` = equal outer margin between each module and its corresponding released carrier edge.

For two equal modules:

`M = (Wc − 2F − g) / 2`

Release only if `M > 0`, left and right margins are equal within 2 mm, the group midpoint is on `C0` within 2 mm, and the two hub heights differ by no more than 2 mm. Confirm tabs, plug, wire bend, guards, fan sweep and vibration clearance; do not use the former unverified `C0 ± 133 mm` rule unless the physical parts and fixture independently produce it.

Fan group installed-airflow target through the finished guard, split auxiliary plane and R0 at 13.5 V:

- **minimum acceptance target: 3,000 m³/h aggregate**;
- **preferred margin: 3,300 m³/h or more aggregate**.

Free-air catalogue values do not prove this target.

## 5. Side-by-side auxiliary plane

Make one shallow, removable split carrier immediately behind the front fan carrier:

- one zone holds the CAC behind the boost/IAT-led fan;
- the other holds the serviceable A/C condenser behind the A/C-led fan;
- a sealed vertical divider and edge seals prevent the fans bypassing their respective core zones;
- both zones discharge directly into the full-width R0 face behind them;
- neither auxiliary core carries the other, R0, a fan or an electrical box;
- every core and fan remains separately removable without cutting.

Choose left/right orientation only after turbo pipe route, A/C ports/drier, bonnet latch, steering and electrical service envelopes are physically represented. Keep both auxiliary cores and their ports inside `W0`; no extra side width is authorised.

## 6. Exact top mounting interface

R0 is located at the **intended top holes**, not to the upright side faces.

At each side:

1. the existing grey upright ends in an inward-facing **horizontal top-return plate** with an existing circular hole;
2. one short black removable radiator-side ear rises from the corresponding R0 side rail and sits directly beneath that horizontal plate;
3. one vertical M8 bolt head and large washer sit visibly on top of the grey plate;
4. the bolt passes down through the **exact existing top hole**, a sleeved EPDM/rubber isolator, then the black radiator ear, with washer and locking nut below; and
5. the radiator ear touches/locates through the isolator directly under the return: no visual air gap, no side-face substitute bolt, no new chassis hole, slot or reaming.

Make a rigid 1:1 two-hole template `B0` recording centre-to-centre, hole diameter/condition, edge distance, plate thickness and elevation. Drill only the removable radiator ears after template transfer. The two lower rubber saddles carry radiator weight; the top bolts locate and restrain it without hard-clamping tank movement.

> *Dono grey top plates ke asal suraakh istemal karo. Bolt upar se seedha neeche jaye; black radiator ear bilkul usi suraakh ke neeche ho. Side mein bolt laga kar tasveer poori na samjho. Neechay rubber saddles wazan uthayen.*

## 7. Electrical and control

Both front fans push grille-to-engine. Keep independently fused branches in the existing covered relay/MIDI system; size relay, MIDI/fuse, cable, connector and earth from measured start/run current and voltage drop. Keep the carrier aligned within the existing front-view silhouette, with closed covers, down/rear cable exits and service access. No side tower or exposed fuse hardware.

Normal lead logic:

- A/C pressure/demand leads the condenser-zone fan;
- boost/high IAT leads the CAC-zone fan;
- coolant temperature may stage either fan; and
- high coolant temperature, high IAT, high A/C pressure, high load, sensor/control fault or manual emergency override commands **both** front fans.

Final thresholds belong to the electrical/engine calibration release. Fan control must fail safe and must not depend on the rear mechanical fan stopping.

## 8. Thermal and mechanical acceptance

Design targets:

- radiator heat rejection: **≥115 kW continuous and ≥130 kW for 10 minutes** at the defined 50 °C duty;
- post-CAC intake-air temperature: **≤80 °C** at the agreed 150 bhp/boost test point;
- CAC route pressure drop: **≤10 kPa** at that point;
- no boost or engine-load derate caused by the cooling system within the accepted 150 bhp envelope.

Before final manufacture, measure `W0`, `P0`, R0, both auxiliary cores, the complete matching fans, B0/top returns, lower saddles and engine-fan sweep. Prove the opaque 1:1 mock-up with grille/front panel, bonnet/latch, hoses, charge pipes, A/C ports/drier, covered electrical carrier and service removals represented.

Final acceptance is instrumented hot-idle A/C and loaded testing at 50 °C ambient, or a documented equivalent heat-soak/load simulation followed by the hottest available road test. Log ambient, coolant inlet/outlet, pre/post-CAC IAT, boost, EGT, fan commands, system voltage, each fan current and pressure/flow results. Reject progressive temperature rise, boiling/loss, hose collapse, contact, electrical overheating, uncontrolled IAT or airflow below target. Correct and repeat the test.

No render or nominal dimension guarantees fit or 50 °C performance.

## 9. Minimum fabrication

Fabricate only:

- one centred matched-pair front fan carrier/shroud;
- one split side-by-side CAC/condenser carrier with divider, seals and isolators;
- two short R0 top ears/adapters copied from B0 and two lower rubber saddles/repairs as needed;
- a rear shroud repair/adapter only if the retained shroud cannot seal R0; and
- measured tabs, P-clips, edging and covered electrical brackets within the existing silhouette.

Never put ties through fins, weld/drill a tank/header/core, hang a component from another heat exchanger, force bolt alignment, or cut new chassis holes.
