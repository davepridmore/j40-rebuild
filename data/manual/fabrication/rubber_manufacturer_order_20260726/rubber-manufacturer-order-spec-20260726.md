## Material

- New black solid automotive mount-grade `EPDM` or `NR/SBR`.
- Shore A hardness `60 ±5`; keep all matching pieces from one compound batch.
- No tyre rubber, recycled/crumb rubber, foam, mixed offcuts, used rubber, or unidentified material.

## Tolerances

- Body pad and oval pad outside dimensions: `±1.0`.
- Finished thickness/height: `±0.5`.
- Body-pad bore: `18.0 +0.5/-0.0`; centred within `±0.5`.
- Oval holes and centre spacing: `±0.5`.
- Strip width: `±1.0`; strip thickness: `±0.5`.
- Faces must be flat and parallel within `0.5`. Holes must be cleanly punched, bored, or waterjet cut.
