# J40 dashboard Rev I V44 — factory height with rounded upper corners

**Status: active owner-corrected appearance design. V44 supersedes V43's sharp upper panel transitions. Production metal and vehicle cutting remain HOLD pending direct M1/M2 vehicle traces, the received Pioneer trace and a full-depth rear buck.**

The active [V44 front-on body-colour design](dashboard_rev_i_v44_factory_height_rounded_top_corners.png) uses `photos/20260413_040719.jpg` as the structural reference for the original dashboard height and lower edge. The [V44 boundary and mounting diagram](dashboard_rev_i_v44_factory_height_rounded_top_corners_boundary.svg) records the intended topology. Neither file is dimensional or suitable for scaling.

## Selected front-face design

- Retain the complete asymmetric silver OEM glovebox, instruction plate, knob, opening gap, hinges and latch unchanged.
- Form one visibly closed body-colour centre panel between the glovebox and speedometer. It must not continue across the driver side or to the far-right dashboard end.
- Begin beside the glovebox and finish at the speedometer-side boundary, with the two side joints visually balanced around the screen and controls. The direct vehicle traces control their final unequal details.
- Blend the top edge into both side joints with smooth, matching OEM-style radii. The upper-left radius should echo the glovebox lid and the upper-right radius should echo the speedometer aperture; do not use sharp points or diagonal notches.
- Keep the replacement entirely inside the original pressed dashboard silhouette. Its bottom is the untouched factory lower edge with **0 mm downward extension**. No apron, skirt, chin, console or new lower band is permitted.
- Consume and remove the small silver ashtray, central rectangular opening and lower slot inside the replacement area. None receives a separate bezel.
- Mount the Pioneer DMH-AP6650BT so its front glass/bezel is coplanar with the new skin.
- Centre exactly seven bought black selectors followed by one separate red hazard in one straight row below the screen.
- Use body-colour warm ivory/cream for the first prototype. No raised pod, floating tablet surround or decorative trim.

## Boundary and construction rule

1. With the glovebox, cluster, column and scallop installed, trace the glovebox right edge, factory upper fold, speedometer upper-left edge and normal lower dashboard edge onto a rigid full-size transparent sheet.
2. Establish the upper corner radii from tangent arcs fitted between the traced top and side boundaries. Use the largest visually consistent radii that preserve the screen carrier, joint land and retained OEM openings; do not infer the radii from this rendering.
3. Establish the visual centre axis of the usable traced field, then balance the screen and controls within it. Do not move either OEM boundary merely to force mathematical symmetry.
4. Prove the 2–3 mm target finished joints after primer/paint, together with glovebox opening/removal sweep and cluster/column service access.
5. Install a continuous hidden 1.5 mm formed-steel backing/Z-frame before trimming the original skin. Target a 20 mm landing flange wherever the rear package permits.
6. Form the visible replacement from 1.2–1.5 mm steel and support it with concealed serviceable M5 fasteners or studs.
7. Tie a separate Pioneer cradle into the backing frame so screen mass and touch loads bypass the face sheet.
8. Prove connector bends, loom strain relief, ventilation and removal order in a rigid full-depth buck before paint or vehicle cutting.

## Dimensional limits

- Pioneer published front/nose reference: approximately 229 W × 131 H × 13 D mm.
- Pioneer published main-chassis reference: approximately 188 W × 108 H × 37 D mm.
- These figures do not establish exact corner radii, aperture, mounting points, connector envelope or installed projection; the received unit controls.
- Every seam and radius in the V44 image and diagram is illustrative. Do not derive coordinates by scaling pixels.
