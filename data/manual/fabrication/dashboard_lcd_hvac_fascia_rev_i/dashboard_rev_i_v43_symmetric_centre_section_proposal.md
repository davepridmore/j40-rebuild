# J40 dashboard Rev I V43 — symmetric centre section, zero drop

**Status: active owner-corrected appearance design. V43 supersedes V42's visually open-ended right side and V41's approximate glovebox-side seam. Production metal and vehicle cutting remain HOLD pending direct M1/M2 vehicle traces, the received Pioneer trace and a full-depth rear buck.**

The active [V43 front-on body-colour design](dashboard_rev_i_v43_symmetric_glovebox_to_speedo_no_drop.png) uses `photos/20260413_040719.jpg` as its straight-on camera and dashboard reference. The [V43 boundary and mounting diagram](dashboard_rev_i_v43_symmetric_boundary_and_mounting.svg) records the intended topology. Neither file is dimensional or suitable for scaling.

## Selected front-face design

- Retain the complete asymmetric silver OEM glovebox, instruction plate, knob, opening gap, hinges and latch unchanged.
- Make one visibly closed, body-colour centre panel between the glovebox and speedometer. It must not continue across the driver side or to the far-right dashboard end.
- Start the upper-left corner at the glovebox's upper-right corner. Run the left service seam immediately beside the glovebox's curved/sloping right edge, with only a directly proved 2–3 mm target finished gap.
- End the upper-right corner at the upper-left/top start of the retained speedometer aperture. Run the right service seam inward toward the panel centre as it descends to the factory lower dashboard edge.
- Make the left and right side cuts visually balanced and approximately mirrored around the screen/control composition, while allowing the direct glovebox and speedometer traces to control the final unequal details.
- Keep the replacement entirely inside the original pressed dashboard silhouette. Its bottom is the untouched factory lower edge with **0 mm downward extension**; no apron, chin or console is permitted.
- Consume and remove the small silver ashtray, central rectangular opening and lower slot inside the replacement area. None receives a separate bezel.
- Mount the Pioneer DMH-AP6650BT so its front glass/bezel is coplanar with the new skin.
- Centre exactly seven bought black selectors followed by one separate red hazard in one straight row below the screen.
- Use body-colour warm ivory/cream for the first prototype. No raised pod, rounded plaque, floating tablet surround or decorative trim.

## Boundary and construction rule

1. With the glovebox, cluster, column and scallop installed, trace the glovebox right edge, factory upper fold, speedometer upper-left edge and normal lower dashboard edge onto a rigid full-size transparent sheet.
2. Establish the visual centre axis of the usable traced field, then balance the screen and controls within it. Do not move either OEM boundary merely to force mathematical symmetry.
3. Prove the 2–3 mm target finished joints after primer/paint, together with glovebox opening/removal sweep and cluster/column service access.
4. Install a continuous hidden 1.5 mm formed-steel backing/Z-frame before trimming the original skin. Target a 20 mm landing flange wherever the rear package permits.
5. Form the visible replacement from 1.2–1.5 mm steel and support it with concealed serviceable M5 fasteners or studs.
6. Tie a separate Pioneer cradle into the backing frame so screen mass and touch loads bypass the face sheet.
7. Prove connector bends, loom strain relief, ventilation and removal order in a rigid full-depth buck before paint or vehicle cutting.

## Dimensional limits

- Pioneer published front/nose reference: approximately 229 W × 131 H × 13 D mm.
- Pioneer published main-chassis reference: approximately 188 W × 108 H × 37 D mm.
- These figures do not establish exact corner radii, aperture, mounting points, connector envelope or installed projection; the received unit controls.
- Every seam in the V43 image and diagram is illustrative. Do not derive coordinates by scaling pixels.
