The replacement consumes the original ashtray pressing and the adjacent rectangular opening completely. Its visible left seam follows the glovebox's curved/sloping right-hand line; its upper and lower seams follow the existing dashboard lines; and it continues rightward to a termination outside the supplied photograph.

## Governing perimeter

1. **Upper seam:** follow the existing upper dashboard face/fold from the point immediately right of the glovebox and continue toward the right-hand dash end.
2. **Glovebox-side seam:** descend beside and parallel to the complete right-hand glovebox outline, preserving the glovebox, latch, hinges and full opening/removal sweep.
3. **Lower seam:** turn around the lower end of the glovebox-side line and follow the existing rising lower dashboard edge toward the right.
4. **Right termination:** the owner's marked photograph deliberately runs out of frame. Do not invent a vertical end line from the rendering. M2 must trace the intended termination against the speedometer/cluster, column structure and dash end in the vehicle.
5. The small silver ashtray, its flange and the adjacent black rectangular opening are all inside the replacement area. They are removed rather than surrounded by separate bezels.

The correct outline is therefore an asymmetric, full-height rightward replacement section. It is not a rounded plaque, rectangular cassette, isolated screen bezel or four-sided panel floating inside the ashtray opening.

## Removable-panel construction

The owner's red line describes the desired **visible joint**. It is not a CNC cut master because the photograph has perspective distortion and the hand-drawn stroke has finite width.

1. Trace the desired visible joint directly on clear sheet with the dashboard, glovebox, cluster and steering column installed in their true positions.
2. Prove a 2–3 mm target finished service gap along the glovebox-side, upper and lower seams. Verify paint build and the glovebox opening/removal sweep.
3. Before releasing the final skin, brace the dash and install a continuous rear backing structure along the future cut. Use formed 1.5 mm steel Z-flanges or equivalent angle/hat sections tied into sound original structure; target 20 mm support land where packaging permits.
4. Trim the original skin to the signed joint trace. The new 1.2–1.5 mm formed steel section lands on the hidden backing frame, so the new and retained faces remain coplanar rather than overlapping on the cabin side.
5. Use concealed, serviceable M5 captive fasteners or studs from the rear/underside. Fastener access and removal order must be demonstrated in the buck; do not depend on adhesive or the Pioneer chassis to carry the panel.
6. Tie a separate receiver cradle into the backing frame. The Pioneer face remains flush with the replacement skin, but screen loads bypass the visible sheet.
7. Add non-load-bearing EPDM snubbers, loom strain relief and protected edge treatment. Prove connector bends, ventilation, drainage exposure and screen/panel removal before paint.

See the [boundary and mounting diagram](dashboard_rev_i_v40_cut_boundary_and_mounting.svg).

## Screen and controls

- Purchased receiver: Pioneer DMH-AP6650BT.

- Published main chassis reference: approximately 188 W × 108 H × 37 D mm; the full connector, cable-bend, cradle and ventilation envelope controls the rear buck.
- Screen face: flush and coplanar with the replacement panel.
- Controls: seven bought black selectors plus one separate red hazard in one restrained row below the screen, subject to the signed right-side boundary and actual rear clearance.
- The replacement remains factory-height and must not hang below the owner's lower cut line.

## Immediate fabrication sequence

1. Make a transparent full-size trace of the owner's three visible boundary runs: upper, glovebox-side and lower.
2. Extend the trace on the vehicle to establish the missing right termination.
3. Make a full replacement-section template in card or 3 mm MDF and confirm glovebox, cluster and steering-column service.
4. Measure the received Pioneer and build its full-depth dummy envelope.
5. Build the hidden backing-frame and receiver-cradle buck before cutting the original dash.
