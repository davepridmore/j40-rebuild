## A/C outlet target interface control

Keep the outlet interfaces separate in every drawing, quote and inspection record:

| Interface | Target used by V32 | Production instruction |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Visible front face, `D_FACE` | Ø87.0 mm | Appearance/proportion envelope; radius 43.5 mm |
| Rear mounting boss or barrel, `D_MOUNT_MAX` | Ø75.0 mm target maximum | Measure every received part at several clock and axial positions; this is not yet a production aperture |
| Louver-body projection, `P_BODY` | ≤ approximately 22 mm | Measure from the finished rear fascia plane to the end of the louver body; record retainer, spigot, clamp and first bend separately |
| Production panel aperture, `D_CUT` | Not released | Define from the measured retention system and a successful same-material, same-thickness, final-finish coupon |
| Hose spigot/adapter, `D_SPIGOT` | Unknown | Measure OD, ID, projection, bead and usable hose engagement; Ø75 mounting data must never be used as a hose size |

If—and only if—the received outlet is a slip-through, back-retained design and its measured maximum boss diameter is no greater than 75.00 mm, the first coupon may use `D_CUT = Ø75.50 mm +0.25 / −0.00`. A snap, interference, threaded or clip-retained design instead uses the retention manufacturer's requirement or a physically proven coupon size. The coupon must be made in the same 1.5 mm CR4 sheet, with the same edge preparation, primer and final paint build as the production fascia.

At the provisional LCD active-image size, each Ø87 face is **43.74% of active width** and **77.76% of active height**. Those ratios control only the V32 front-view visualization; CAD coordinates come from the signed vehicle trace and received-part measurements.

## Mandatory clearances and routing

- At least 20 mm from every new fixed part, duct and wire to the signed moving column/shroud/stalk envelope through full movement.
- At least 10 mm between vent retainers, necks, elbows or ducts and fixed LCD, cluster, glovebox hardware, selector contact stacks, terminals, loom and structural hardware.
- Where the Ø87 visible face and a 10 mm visible gap control, keep an outlet centre at least **53.5 mm** from a glovebox-lid edge, unsupported fascia boundary or adjacent visible component. Where only a conditional Ø75.5 mm trial aperture and 10 mm structural land control, keep the centre at least **47.75 mm** from that boundary; the stricter 53.5 mm face rule controls wherever both apply.
- Keep each outer outlet wholly on fixed fascia. The passenger outlet and rear package must remain outside the complete glovebox opening sweep.
- Support both duct branches. No duct section may be crushed below 90% of its round inside diameter; use the largest practical bend radius and prove balanced useful airflow at both ends.
- Preserve an original or dedicated windscreen-demist path; the two occupant outlets are not the sole demist provision.
- Preserve cabin-side service removal for the LCD, both vents, the control bank and evaporator connections.
