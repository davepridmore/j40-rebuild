# J40 dashboard Rev I V41 — front-on full-section design

**Status: superseded by V43. V41 transferred the accepted V40 oblique concept onto the project straight-on photograph, but its glovebox-side seam does not control the current design. Retain it as appearance history only.**

The active [front-on body-colour design](dashboard_rev_i_v41_front_on_full_section_body_colour.png) uses `photos/20260413_040719.jpg` as its camera and dashboard reference. The earlier [V40 body-colour view](dashboard_rev_i_v40_full_ashtray_section_body_colour.png) controls design intent only: it established that the work is one full-height replacement skin rather than a screen surround placed inside the ashtray opening.

## Selected front-face design

- Retain the complete asymmetric silver OEM glovebox, Japanese instruction plate and black knob unchanged.
- Begin one removable replacement skin immediately alongside the glovebox's right-hand curved/sloping line, with a narrow service joint.
- Follow the original upper dashboard fold and the original lower dashboard edge; add no drop below the factory line.
- Finish at the directly traced left boundary of the retained factory instrument-cluster, steering-column and scallop structure.
- Consume and remove the small silver ashtray, central rectangular opening and lower rectangular slot as old features inside the replacement area. None receives a separate bezel.
- Recess the Pioneer DMH-AP6650BT so its front glass/bezel is coplanar with the new skin.
- Use exactly seven bought black selectors and one separate red hazard in one straight line below the screen.
- Use body-colour warm ivory/cream for the first prototype. No raised pod, rounded plaque, floating black tablet surround or dropped console.

## Boundary and construction rule

The [V41 boundary and mounting diagram](dashboard_rev_i_v41_cut_boundary_and_mounting.svg) explains the topology, but neither it nor the generated photograph is a cut template. Perspective, generative reconstruction and the incomplete physical survey prevent dimensional release.

1. Trace the glovebox, upper fold, lower edge, cluster boundary and column/scallop directly on a transparent full-size sheet with the parts installed.
2. Prove a 2–3 mm target finished service gap, paint build, glovebox opening/removal sweep and cluster/column service access.
3. Install a continuous hidden 1.5 mm formed-steel backing/Z-frame before trimming the original skin. Target a 20 mm landing flange wherever the rear package permits.
4. Form the visible replacement from 1.2–1.5 mm steel and support it with concealed serviceable M5 fasteners or studs.
5. Tie a separate Pioneer cradle into the backing frame so screen mass and touch loads bypass the face sheet.
6. Prove actual connector bends, loom strain relief, ventilation and removal order in a rigid full-depth buck before paint or vehicle cutting.

## Dimensional limits

- Pioneer published front/nose reference: approximately 229 W × 131 H × 13 D mm.
- Pioneer published main-chassis reference: approximately 188 W × 108 H × 37 D mm.
- These figures do not establish exact corner radii, aperture, mounting points, connector envelope or installed projection; the received unit controls.
- Every seam in the front-on rendering is illustrative. Do not derive coordinates by scaling pixels.

The next fabrication artifact should be a clear/card/MDF full-size boundary template plus a separate complete Pioneer dummy envelope—not production steel.
