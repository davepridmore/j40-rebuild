# J40 dashboard Rev I V35 — production CNC measurement checklist

This is the field-survey list needed to convert the selected V35 centre-cassette dashboard into an exact, production-cuttable CNC model. Record the results in `cnc_measurement_schedule.csv` and attach a photograph, scan, drawing or signed template for every completed row.

The current `dashboard_rev_i_v35_quotation_cnc_spec.md` and its scale-controlled provisional elevation are deliberately **quotation-only assumption models**. They can be used to estimate material, programming, forming and prototype costs; they must not be used to cut the vehicle or production metal.

## Survey setup and coordinate system

- Install the original glovebox, complete speedometer cluster, steering column/shroud/stalks, purchased wheel and boss before tracing.
- Remove the ashtray only after its aperture and surrounding structure are recorded. The V35 removable centre cassette covers and bridges this traced centre service opening; retain its designed service seam and concealed fixings.
- Hold a rigid flat survey frame parallel to the intended finished fascia plane. Establish `Z = 0` at the finished front surface.
- Use the signed top-left point of the complete finished fascia face as origin: `X = 0`, `Y = 0`. `X` increases from passenger side to driver side on this RHD vehicle; `Y` increases downward; `Z` increases rearward.
- Mark the complete-face midpoint, steering-shaft axis and at least four body reference targets that will survive panel removal. Record both diagonals so the trace can be checked for scale and squareness.
- Use a perpendicular camera/scan position and place two certified scale bars in the fascia plane. A photograph alone is secondary evidence; a signed 1:1 trace or scan is the production master.

## Minimum equipment and recording resolution

| Tool | Minimum use | Record to |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Steel rule/tape and rigid straightedge | Overall fascia, structure and duct runs | 1.0 mm |
| Digital caliper | Component bodies, bosses, apertures, sheet and paint | 0.1 mm |
| Angle finder/inclinometer | Fascia, column, outlet and HVAC port axes | 0.5 degrees |
| Radius gauges/profile comb/card templates | Corners, folds, scallop and glovebox/cluster profiles | 0.5 mm |
| Depth gauge/rigid depth rods | Rear envelopes and obstructions | 1.0 mm |
| 1:1 paper/Mylar/card trace | Complete face, openings and mounting points | 1.0 mm |

Every dimension is measured at least twice. A second person checks the origin, overall width/height, diagonals, screen centre and steering axis before sign-off.

## Exact measurements required

### M1 — complete fascia, mounting and forming

1. Full outer profile as ordered `X,Y` points or a vector scan, including top fold, both ends, the normal lower edge and every local step/radius.
2. Overall finished-face width and height, plus lower-edge `Y` at no fewer than five evenly spaced `X` stations.
3. Face `Z` offset/curvature on a grid and at every new or retained component centre.
4. Existing sheet thickness, proposed new sheet thickness, paint/primer build and any doubled/reinforced areas.
5. Every top, bottom and end return: start line, developed width, bend angle, inside radius and direction.
6. Every body fixing/support point: centre coordinates, hole/slot size, fastener, stand-off, access direction and surrounding no-cut land.
7. Structural members, windscreen mounts, demist structure, captive nuts, loom clips and body seams behind the face; identify immutable/no-cut zones.
8. Proposed vehicle cut boundary and support/reinforcement method, signed by the body shop before metal removal.

### M2 — retained glovebox and ashtray deletion

9. Glovebox lid and aperture profiles separately, including every corner radius and the installed gap around the lid.
10. Glovebox profile position from the main origin; hinge pivots, latch/striker, black knob, Japanese plate and mounting-hole coordinates.
11. Glovebox box rear envelope and its full opening/removal swept envelope, including latch and hinge projections.
12. Ashtray aperture, supports and conflicts; record which parts are removed and which structural lips must remain before the opening is closed flush.

### M3 — retained cluster, steering column and driver controls

13. Complete cluster bezel and panel-aperture profiles, mounting holes/studs and installed gaps.
14. Cluster rear envelope, connector locations, cable bend space and cabin-side removal path.
15. Steering-shaft centre at the fascia plane, shaft diameter and shaft axis angle in plan and elevation.
16. Exact factory column scallop/U-profile and its relationship to the cluster lower lip.
17. Column shroud, stalks, boss, purchased wheel and hands-on controls through their complete tilt/rotation/operation swept envelopes.
18. Knee/leg clearance envelope at pedals and normal driving position.

### M4/M5 — purchased Pioneer DMH-AP6650BT and its carrier

The seller and manufacturer publish an approximately 188 W × 108 H × 37 D mm main chassis. Treat that as a planning input only: it does not establish the display/bezel outline, visible aperture, mounts, connector package or complete installed projection.

19. Confirm the received model/part number and controlled drawing if available; measure the display/bezel outer width, height, corner radii and projection separately from the main chassis.
20. Active-image width/height and its four offsets from the display edges; verify the marketed 9-inch diagonal rather than assuming the provisional 198.91 × 111.89 mm rectangle.
21. Required visible aperture/bezel overlap, touch dead border and acceptable edge reveal.
22. Mounting-hole/thread/clip pattern, carrier thickness and installation/removal direction.
23. Confirm the approximately 188 × 108 × 37 mm main chassis and map the rear chassis, heat sink and every connector used by the installed loom—including phone USB, microphone, front/rear/subwoofer RCA, reverse-camera/trigger, power/speaker loom and amplifier remote where fitted—with plug bodies, minimum bend radii and service loops.
24. Ventilation/drain requirements and required clearance from metal, HVAC ducting and wiring; commission initially on the receiver's four speaker channels while reserving a serviceable RCA/remote/power path for an optional compact external amplifier.

### M6 — seven selectors and separate hazard control

25. Measure all seven received selectors individually: required panel cut, thread/key/anti-rotation detail, bezel/head diameter and clamped sheet range.
26. Lever/handle sweep for each 2- and 3-position selector and the required hand/finger clearance.
27. Contact-block rear envelope, terminal positions, wire entry, service loop and removal direction for each control.
28. Repeat the same measurement set for the separate red hazard control.
29. Confirm engraved/printed label extents, centre-to-centre pitch and edge margins for the single line of seven selectors plus separate red hazard.
30. Record electrical contact truth tables for every selected position before loom manufacture.

### M7 — all four received circular A/C outlets

31. Label V1–V4 and measure visible face OD at multiple clock positions; record minimum, maximum and ovality.
32. Measure rear boss/barrel OD at multiple clock positions and at the front, middle and rear axial stations.
33. Identify retention method and measure tabs, clips, threads, keys, shoulders, bezel overlap and anti-rotation features.
34. Measure rearward louver-body depth from the finished rear fascia plane, then record retainer projection separately.
35. Measure duct spigot OD, ID, usable engagement length, bead/barb, clamp land and total rear projection separately from the mounting boss.
36. Record outlet aim range, shutoff movement and the moving rear swept envelope.
37. Trial the selected hose, clamp and first elbow; record assembled maximum OD, required straight engagement and minimum bend radius.
38. Make at least three same-material coupons around the predicted cut size, apply the production coating stack and prove retention/removal without cracking or looseness.
39. Select the closest-matched pair for installation and retain the other two, labelled and bagged, as service spares.

### M8 — evaporator, demist and complete duct system

40. Exact evaporator case width, height and depth, plus brackets, fittings, blower/intake, drain and service/removal envelopes.
41. Both supply takeoff port OD/ID, centre positions, axis directions, usable engagement, beads and clamp lands.
42. Left and right duct routes as centreline points with segment lengths, bends, support points, maximum OD and obstruction clearance.
43. Windscreen demist takeoff, branch size, route and outlet; the two occupant outlets cannot be the only demist provision.
44. Condensate drain fall, hose size and firewall/floor exit.

### M9/M10 — prototype, electrical and release proof

45. Full-size face template fit, with all edges, retained openings and body holes checked against the vehicle.
46. Full-depth rigid buck with LCD, vents/ducts, selectors, cluster, glovebox and column installed simultaneously.
47. Minimum fixed clearance at every closest approach; requirement is at least 10 mm after tolerance, trim and service movement.
48. Minimum clearance to the complete moving column/shroud/stalk envelope; requirement is at least 20 mm.
49. Glovebox, column/stalks, wheel, pedals, every selector, vent aim/shutoff and all service-removal paths exercised without contact.
50. Two-outlet airflow, temperature, noise, demist, drain and supported-duct tests with the actual evaporator and complete branches installed.
51. Selector truth tables, relays/controller logic, wiper park, lights, blower OFF/LOW/HIGH, A/C safety/fan logic, hazard isolation, FUEL STOP RUN/STOP, key-off and manual-stop fallback tested and signed.

## Release package the CNC supplier must receive

- Signed 1:1 face trace/scan and a dimensioned coordinate export using the datum above.
- Completed `cnc_measurement_schedule.csv` with evidence references and owner/body-shop sign-off.
- Manufacturer drawings or signed measured drawings for LCD, vents, selectors and hazard.
- Approved full-size face-template and full-depth-buck inspection sheets.
- Final flat pattern and formed-part drawing showing material, grain, bends, radii, K-factor/bend allowance, tolerances, finish and feature IDs.
- Final DXF in millimetres at 1:1 plus a human-readable PDF. The CNC supplier must compare DXF extents to the stated overall dimensions before cutting.

Production release requires every row marked `production_blocker` in the schedule to be `measured_pass` or `approved_by_coupon`. Blank cells, raster-render pixels and assumed coordinates are not measurement evidence.
