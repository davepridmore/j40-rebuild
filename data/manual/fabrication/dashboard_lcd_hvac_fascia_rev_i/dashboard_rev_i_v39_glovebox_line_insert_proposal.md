# J40 dashboard Rev I V39 — glovebox-line centre insert

**Status: superseded appearance study. The owner's marked cut boundary is now recorded in [V40](dashboard_rev_i_v40_full_ashtray_section_proposal.md), which replaces the complete ashtray/right-hand skin section rather than using this narrower centre-insert perimeter.**

V39 treats the original dashboard as the primary shape. The removable panel occupies only the existing centre bay between the OEM glovebox and the OEM speedometer/cluster. Its left edge runs alongside the glovebox's right-hand seam; its right edge follows the cluster's left-hand seam; its top and bottom follow the existing upper and lower dashboard lines. It is not a rounded plaque around the receiver and it is not a full-width replacement fascia.

## Correct perimeter rule

The panel outline is an offset of four existing vehicle datums, not an arbitrary geometric shape:

1. **Left:** run the visible service gap consistently alongside the complete right-hand glovebox seam. Do not bridge onto the glovebox, square its curve or move it for visual balance.
2. **Right:** run the service gap alongside the complete left-hand cluster seam and stop before the cluster and steering-column scallop.
3. **Top:** follow the centre section of the original upper dashboard face/fold. Retain enough original steel and hidden reinforcement to carry the insert and screen.
4. **Bottom:** meet the existing full-width lower dashboard line with zero downward extension. Do not create a hanging centre pod.

The resulting face is a context-derived, slightly tapered four-sided panel. Small corner radii come only from the measured intersections and the selected cutting/forming process. Do not substitute a rounded rectangle, capsule, oval, hexagon, isolated bezel or symmetric trapezoid.

## What remains unchanged

- OEM glovebox door, opening, hinges, latch, plate, knob and full opening/removal sweep.
- OEM speedometer/cluster, its opening, directly traced position, steering-column axis and scallop.
- Full dashboard upper fold, full-width lower edge, outer/end contours and both outer outlets.
- Pioneer screen position, one-line control allocation and red hazard position from the V37 appearance study, subject to actual-part measurement.
- V37 flush-screen support principle: the black screen face is coplanar with the insert face and is carried by an adjustable rear cradle, not by the visible sheet alone.

## Cut and support strategy

The visible panel edge and the structural vehicle cut are different lines.

1. Trace the four vehicle datums directly with the dashboard, glovebox and cluster installed in their true positions.
2. Make a full-size transparent template of the desired 2–3 mm visible perimeter gap. Verify the glovebox sweep, cluster service route, column/shroud clearance and control access.
3. Define a smaller structural opening behind that visible outline. Retain a continuous original-steel landing flange, nominally 15 mm minimum and 20 mm preferred, wherever the physical dashboard permits. No production cut is released where the trace cannot retain sound material.
4. Add a welded or bonded rear perimeter frame with offset Z-tabs so the removable insert face sits in the same local plane as the retained dash. The insert must not lie on top of the dashboard face.
5. Use concealed rear M5 studs or captive fasteners on the perimeter frame. Make the complete insert, receiver and control loom removable from the cabin side without disturbing the glovebox or cluster.
6. Tie the screen aperture doubler and the adjustable receiver U-cradle into the rear perimeter frame. Use the V37 trial aperture only for mock-up; the received Pioneer controls the production opening.
7. Add non-load-bearing EPDM snubbers and separate loom strain relief. Prove service loops, connector bends, ventilation and the removal sequence in the full-depth buck.

## Finish-specific notes

- **Body colour:** 1.5 mm CR4 mild steel remains the simplest production route. Paint the insert separately and tune the visible gap with the final coating build included.
- **Charcoal:** use the same steel construction with fine-texture satin powder coat. Mask all earth points and captive-fastener interfaces.
- **Walnut:** use veneer only as a thin cosmetic laminate on a stable aluminium, phenolic or sealed steel carrier. Balance the rear face, seal every edge, use heat/humidity-rated adhesive and a UV-stable satin clear coat. Do not use a solid timber panel. Account for the laminate thickness when setting the screen coplanarity.

## Recommendation

Proceed first with the **body-colour V39A full-size clear template and MDF/cheap-plastic buck** because its seams reveal whether the glovebox-line geometry is correct without a contrasting finish dominating the judgment. Use the same accepted perimeter for charcoal or walnut; the finish decision does not reopen the vehicle cut geometry.
