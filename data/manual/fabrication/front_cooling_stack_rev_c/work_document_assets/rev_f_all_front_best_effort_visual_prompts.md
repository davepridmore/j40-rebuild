## Status and governing caveat

These photorealistic images communicate Rev F design intent, simplicity and appearance. They are not dimensional proof. The controlled drawings, the real M1–M6 measurements, the physical dummy assembly, and the U1–U7/F1–F4/E1–E2/T1–T3 acceptance checks govern fabrication and release. Where an image conflicts with a measured result or controlled drawing, the measured result or controlled drawing takes precedence.
