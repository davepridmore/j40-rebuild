# Rev I refurbishment visual set

These images explain the intended arrangement of the refurbished original J40
radiator and the added charge-air (turbo) cooling stage. They are visual aids,
not dimensionally reliable fabrication drawings. The physical R0 radiator,
vehicle hard points, and Rev I drawings D11-D13 are the fabrication authority.

## Source evidence

- `photos/20260802_173643_gp_tJNrLg8A.jpg` — removed original copper/brass
  engine radiator (R0); geometry, necks, tanks, side ears and repair condition.
- `photos/20260802_173652_gp_XFyn0ruQ.jpg` — removable expanded-mesh stone guard.
- Project archive chassis photographs — existing top-tab holes, lower radiator
  support and engine-side clearance.
- Earlier project renders — composition reference only; superseded by Rev I.

## Image-generation direction

Photorealistic workshop/product photography; preserve the proportions and
recognisable original-radiator construction from R0; exactly three heat
exchangers and exactly three fans; no transparent cutaway; no side electrical
tower; no unused upper mounting holes; simple black steel brackets; parts that
could realistically be fabricated and serviced in Pakistan.

The renders deliberately avoid printed dimensions and part labels because image
generation cannot guarantee exact typography or geometry. Use D11-D13 for all
dimensions, centreline positions, clearances and release checks.
